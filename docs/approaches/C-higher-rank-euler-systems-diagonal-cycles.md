# Approach C — Attack via diagonal cycles, generalised Kato classes, and rank-two phenomena

Status: research file, written 2026-09-11. Follows `../00-charter.md` (tags `[THEOREM]`, `[CONDITIONAL]`, `[CONJECTURE]`, `[HEURISTIC]`, `[NEW]`, `[GAP]`). Every citation below was checked against the arXiv abstract page, the publisher page, or the authors' posted PDF on 2026-09-11 (see §8 for the search log). Theorem numbers are quoted from the posted versions of the papers; where a number could not be checked it is marked `[unverified]`.

## 0. Summary of the outcome

The question posed to this file is whether the only mechanism that has so far produced *explicit, canonical* cohomology classes controlling a rank-two Selmer group — the Darmon–Rotger generalised Kato classes $\kappa(f,g,h)$ built from $p$-adic families of Gross–Kudla–Schoen diagonal cycles — can be made to bear on the Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture for $E/\mathbb{Q}$ in analytic rank $2$, i.e. on the *trivial* Artin representation.

The answer, pushed to its precise [GAP]s, is the following.

1. `[THEOREM]` The trivial representation occurs in $V_g\otimes V_h$ (both cuspidal weight one) exactly when $h=g^*$, and then with multiplicity one; the generalised Kato class $\kappa(f,g_\alpha,g^*_{\alpha^{-1}})$ then has a component $\kappa_p(E)\in H^1(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)$ which is a Selmer class as soon as $L(E,1)=0$ (Darmon–Rotger, Bertolini–Seveso–Venerucci). This is the *only* member of the whole Garrett–Rankin–Selberg family (Kato / Beilinson–Flach / diagonal cycles) in which a class can land in $\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)$ in a genuine rank-two regime (Lemma 3.1, `[NEW]`, elementary).

2. `[THEOREM]` (Castella–Hsieh 2022; Castella 2023, 2026.) When $g$ is a CM theta series (of an imaginary quadratic $K$ in which $p$ splits) and under explicit hypotheses, $\kappa_p(E)\neq 0 \iff \big(\dim_{\mathbb{Q}_p}\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)=2$ and $\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)\not\subset\ker(\mathrm{loc}_p)\big)$, and when nonzero $\kappa_p(E)$ spans the *strict* (fine) Selmer line $\ker(\mathrm{loc}_p)$. Nothing is known for non-CM $g$.

3. The crux (§4.6, Proposition 4.9 `[NEW]`, formal): the nonvanishing of $\kappa_p(E)$ is *equivalent* to the $p$-Selmer form of the conclusion one wants. Hence $\kappa_p(E)$ is a certificate of rank two, not a producer of it: any proof of "$r_{\mathrm{an}}(E)=2\Rightarrow\kappa_p(E)\neq0$" is a proof of "$r_{\mathrm{an}}(E)=2\Rightarrow \operatorname{corank}\mathrm{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/\mathbb{Q})=2$". This is `[GAP C.1]`. The only analytic handle on $\kappa_p(E)$ is the leading term of the *anticyclotomic* $p$-adic $L$-function $\Theta_{f/K}$ of Bertolini–Darmon at the trivial character (Castella–Hsieh, Theorem 5.3), whose relation to $L''(E,1)$ is unknown (`[GAP C.2]`). All $p$-adic local invariants of $\kappa_p(E)$ at $p$ vanish identically (it lies in the fine Selmer group), so no reciprocity law can see it.

4. `[THEOREM]` (§5) Over $\mathbb{Q}$ the Euler-system rank of $T_pE$ is intrinsically $1$ ($\operatorname{rank} H^0(\mathbb{R},T_pE)=1$); every Kolyvagin system for $(T_pE,\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{can}})$ has vanishing bottom class when $\operatorname{rank}E(\mathbb{Q})\ge2$ (Mazur–Rubin structure theorem, Corollary 5.3), and the rank-two Selmer structure is already *controlled* by the derived Kolyvagin classes of Kato's Euler system, which are nonzero (Burungale–Castella–Grossi–Skinner 2023/26). The analytic counterpart of these derived classes is Kurihara's modular-symbol invariants, not $L''(E,1)$. A "rank-two Euler system for $T_pE$ over $\mathbb{Q}$" is not a meaningful object in the Perrin-Riou/Burns–Sano formalism; over an imaginary quadratic $K$ it would be (core rank $2$), and no example is known.

5. `[NEW]` (Proposition 5.5) From Kato's $\Lambda$-adic zeta element one obtains, unconditionally under Kato's hypotheses, a canonical nonzero class $z^{\mathrm{der}}\in\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)$ in *every* rank as soon as $\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)\not\subset\ker(\mathrm{loc}_p)$; it is the first nonvanishing $(\gamma-1)$-adic Taylor coefficient of $z^{\mathrm{Iw}}_{\mathrm{Kato}}$. Conditionally on Perrin-Riou's derivative formula and on $\operatorname{ord}_{T=0}L_p(E,T)=2$, the pair $(\kappa_p(E),z^{\mathrm{der}})$ is a basis of a rank-two Selmer group — the exact analogue over $\mathbb{Q}$ of Castella's basis $(\kappa_p,x^{(2)}_{\mathfrak p})$ for CM curves. This still does not touch $L''(E,1)$.

## Notation

$E/\mathbb{Q}$ elliptic curve of conductor $N=N_f$, $f\in S_2(\Gamma_0(N_f))$ its newform, $V_f=V_pE$, $p\nmid N_f$ a prime of good ordinary reduction, $\alpha_f$ the unit root and $\beta_f=p/\alpha_f$. Two newforms of weight one $g\in S_1(N_g,\chi)$, $h\in S_1(N_h,\chi^{-1})$ with Deligne–Serre Artin representations $\varrho_g,\varrho_h:G_\mathbb{Q}\to \mathrm{GL}_2(L)$, realised on $V_g,V_h$ (over $L\subset\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_p$ after fixing embeddings); $V_{gh}:=V_g\otimes V_h$, $V_{fgh}:=V_f\otimes V_{gh}$ (an $8$-dimensional Kummer self-dual representation). $H$ the field cut out by $V_{gh}$. Frobenius eigenvalues on $V_g$: $\alpha_g,\beta_g$ (roots of $x^2-a_p(g)x+\chi(p)$), similarly $\alpha_h,\beta_h$; $g_\alpha=g(z)-\beta_g g(pz)$ etc. the $p$-stabilisations. $\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V)$ the Bloch–Kato Selmer group; $\mathrm{Sel}_0(\mathbb{Q},V_pE):=\ker\big(\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)\xrightarrow{\mathrm{loc}_p}H^1(\mathbb{Q}_p,V_pE)\big)$ the strict (fine) Selmer group; $\log_p:E(\mathbb{Q}_p)\hat\otimes\mathbb{Q}_p\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathbb{Q}_p$ the formal group logarithm. The convention "$(k,\ell,m)$ balanced" means each weight is strictly smaller than the sum of the other two; $(2,1,1)$ is *unbalanced* with $f$ dominant. $r_{\mathrm{an}}(E,\varrho):=\operatorname{ord}_{s=1}L(E,\varrho,s)$, $r(E,\varrho):=\dim_L\operatorname{Hom}_{G_\mathbb{Q}}(\varrho,E(H)\otimes L)$.

## 1. Diagonal cycles and the triple product $L$-function

### 1.1 Analytic background

`[THEOREM]` (Garrett [Gar87]; Piatetski-Shapiro–Rallis [PSR87].) For newforms $f,g,h$ of weights $k,\ell,m$ the triple product $L$-function $L(f\otimes g\otimes h,s)$ has meromorphic continuation and a functional equation with centre $c=(k+\ell+m-2)/2$; for $(k,\ell,m)=(2,1,1)$ the centre is $s=1$ and $L(f\otimes g\otimes h,s)=L(E,V_{gh},s)$ is the Hasse–Weil–Artin $L$-function of $E$ twisted by $V_{gh}$ (Darmon–Rotger [DR17, §Intro]).

`[THEOREM]` (Prasad [Pra90, Thm. 1.4], as used in [DR17, DR16].) If $\gcd(N_f,N_gN_h)=1$ then all local root numbers of $L(E,V_{gh},s)$ equal $+1$; in particular the global sign is $+1$ and $r_{\mathrm{an}}(E,V_{gh})$ is even. The archimedean sign for weights $(2,1,1)$ is $+1$; for weights $(k,\ell,m)$ in the balanced region (e.g. $(2,2,2)$) it is $-1$ ([DR16, §3.1]; [GK92]; [YZZ23, §1]).

`[THEOREM]` (Harris–Kudla [HK91]: Jacquet's conjecture; Ichino [Ich08]: explicit formula.) $L(f\otimes g\otimes h,c)\neq0$ iff some trilinear period $\int_{[D^\times]}\varphi_1\varphi_2\varphi_3\neq 0$ on a quaternion algebra $D$ determined by the local signs; Ichino gives $|\int\varphi_1\varphi_2\varphi_3|^2$ as an explicit multiple of $L(f\otimes g\otimes h,c)$ by local trilinear forms.

### 1.2 Gross–Kudla–Schoen cycles and the Gross–Kudla conjecture

`[CONJECTURE]` (Gross–Kudla [GK92].) For $f,g,h$ of weight $2$ and square-free level $N$ with global sign $-1$, let $X$ be the Shimura curve attached to the indefinite quaternion algebra ramified at the primes $q\mid N$ with $\epsilon_q=-1$ and $\Delta(F)\in \mathrm{CH}^2(X^3)_0$ the $(f,g,h)$-isotypic component of the Gross–Schoen modified diagonal cycle [GS95]. Then $L'(f\otimes g\otimes h,2)=\Omega(F)\,\langle\Delta(F),\Delta(F)\rangle_{BB}$ with $\Omega(F)>0$ explicit and $\langle\,,\rangle_{BB}$ the Beilinson–Bloch height.

`[THEOREM, preprint]` (Yuan–Zhang–Zhang [YZZ23, Conj. 1.3.1, Thm. 1.3.3], version of 14 Nov 2023, unpublished; W. Zhang's page lists it as "in preparation".) For cuspidal automorphic representations $\pi=\pi_1\otimes\pi_2\otimes\pi_3$ of parallel weight $2$ on an incoherent quaternion algebra over a totally real field $F$ with trivial product of central characters, there are algebraic numbers $c_p$ (for the rational primes $p$ over which $\pi$ is ramified) such that the height pairing of the Gross–Kudla–Schoen cycles satisfies
$$\mathcal{L}(\pi)=\frac{16\,\zeta_F(2)^2}{L(1,\pi,\mathrm{ad})}L'(1/2,\pi)+\sum_{p\in P}c_p\log p .$$
In particular the generalised Gross–Kudla conjecture holds when $\pi$ is everywhere unramified.

**Remark 1.1.** Over $F=\mathbb{Q}$ there are no weight-two cusp forms of level $1$, so for an elliptic curve $E/\mathbb{Q}$ the theorem of [YZZ23] currently gives the Gross–Kudla formula only up to the undetermined term $\sum_{p\mid N}c_p\log p$. `[GAP C.6]` (§6) records this.

### 1.3 Darmon–Rotger I: the $p$-adic Gross–Zagier formula

`[THEOREM]` (Darmon–Rotger [DR14], main theorem; as summarised in [DR16, §3.2] and [DR17, Introduction].) For Hida families $\mathbf f,\mathbf g,\mathbf h$ and *balanced* classical weights $(k,\ell,m)$ (all $\ge2$), the $p$-adic étale Abel–Jacobi image of the generalised Gross–Kudla–Schoen cycle on the product of three Kuga–Sato varieties, paired against $\eta_f\otimes\omega_g\otimes\omega_h$, equals the value at $(k,\ell,m)$ — *outside* the interpolation region — of the $f$-unbalanced Garrett–Rankin (Harris–Tilouine/Hida) $p$-adic $L$-function $\mathcal L_p^{f}(\mathbf f,\mathbf g,\mathbf h)$, whose interpolation region is the $f$-dominant region $k\ge \ell+m$. The interpolation property itself, in the form "$L(f\otimes g\otimes h,1)\neq0$ iff $\mathcal L_p^{f_\alpha}(\breve f^*,\breve g\breve h)(y,z)\neq0$ for some test vectors", is [DR14, Thms. 4.2, 4.7] as quoted in [DR17, proof of Thm. 6.4].

### 1.4 Darmon–Rotger II: generalised Kato classes and BSD in analytic rank zero

Hypotheses in force in [DR17]: $\varrho_g,\varrho_h$ odd irreducible two-dimensional Artin representations with $\det\varrho_g=\det\varrho_h^{-1}$; $\gcd(N_f,N_gN_h)=1$; $p\nmid N:=\operatorname{lcm}(N_f,N_g,N_h)$ odd, $f$ ordinary and not Eisenstein at $p$; $\alpha_g\neq\beta_g$, $\alpha_h\neq\beta_h$ (regularity at $p$).

`[THEOREM]` ([DR17, Thm. A = Thm. 6.7].) If $L(E,\varrho_{gh},1)\neq0$ then $E(H)^{\varrho_{gh}}:=\operatorname{Hom}_{G_\mathbb{Q}}(V_{gh},E(H)\otimes L)=0$. (Here $p$ is *chosen*: the proof picks $p\ge5$ by Chebotarev with $a_p(f)\neq0$ and $\varrho_g,\varrho_h$ regular; the proof does **not** give finiteness of the $\varrho_{gh}$-part of Ш — [DR17, p. 609] states this explicitly.)

`[THEOREM]` ([DR17, Thm. C = Thm. 6.4].) The generalised Kato class $\kappa(f,g_\alpha,h_\alpha)\in H^1(\mathbb{Q},V_{fgh}(N))$, defined as the specialisation at weights $(2,1,1)$ of the $\Lambda$-adic diagonal class (limit of classes $\kappa(f,g_k,h_k)$ with $g_k,h_k$ weight-$k\ge2$ specialisations of Hida families through $g_\alpha,h_\alpha$), is crystalline at $p$ iff $L(f\otimes g\otimes h,1)=0$. The classes are locally trivial at all $\ell\neq p$ ([DR17, after (159)]). The reciprocity law behind this is [DR17, Thm. 5.3]: the image of $\kappa(f,g_\alpha,h_\alpha)$ in the singular quotient $H^1_{\mathrm{sing}}(\mathbb{Q}_p,V_{fgh})$ lies in $H^1_{\mathrm{sing}}(\mathbb{Q}_p,V_f\otimes V^{\beta\beta}_{gh})$, $V^{\beta\beta}_{gh}:=V_g^{\beta}\otimes V_h^{\beta}$, and is computed by $\mathcal L_p^{f}(f,g_\alpha,h_\alpha)(2,1,1)$, which is an interpolated value since $(2,1,1)$ lies in the closure of the $f$-dominant region.

`[THEOREM]` ([DR17, Thms. B, D = Thm. 6.13].) If $L(E,\varrho_{gh},1)=0$ and $\mathcal L_p^{g_\alpha}(\breve f,\breve g^*,\breve h)\neq0$ for some test vectors (a value of the $g$-unbalanced $p$-adic $L$-function at $(2,1,1)$, *outside* its interpolation region), then there are projections $j_1,j_2$ with $\kappa_{\alpha\alpha}=j_1\kappa(f,g_\alpha,h_\alpha)$, $\kappa_{\alpha\beta}=j_2\kappa(f,g_\alpha,h_\beta)$ linearly independent in $\mathrm{Sel}_p(E,\varrho_{gh})$; in particular $\dim\mathrm{Sel}_p(E,\varrho_{gh})\ge2$.

### 1.5 Bertolini–Seveso–Venerucci

`[THEOREM]` ([BSV22a, Thm. A], $p>5$, self-duality $\chi_f\chi_g\chi_h=1$, Assumption 1.1 on weight-one specialisations.) There is a canonical three-variable balanced class $\kappa(\mathbf f,\mathbf g,\mathbf h)\in H^1_{\mathrm{bal}}(\mathbb{Q},\mathbb V(\mathbf f,\mathbf g,\mathbf h))$ such that for each $\xi\in\{f,g,h\}$ the $\xi$-branch of the Perrin-Riou big logarithm satisfies $\mathcal L_\xi(\mathrm{res}_p\kappa)=\mathcal L^\xi_p(\mathbf f,\mathbf g,\mathbf h)$. `[THEOREM]` ([BSV22a, Thm. B].) The (suitably improved, in the two exceptional cases which require $p\,\|\,c(f_2)$) specialisation $\kappa^*(f_k,g_\ell,h_m)$ at an $f$-unbalanced classical triple is crystalline at $p$ iff $L(f_k^\sharp\otimes g^\sharp_\ell\otimes h^\sharp_m,c)=0$ (Assumption 1.7: local signs $+1$). Darmon–Rotger's [DR22a] gives an independent construction of the three-variable class and the reciprocity law via Perrin-Riou's $\Lambda$-adic regulator.

`[THEOREM]` ([BSV22b, Thm. A, Cor. B].) For $A/\mathbb{Q}$ with *multiplicative* reduction at $p>3$, $K$ quadratic with $p$ inert, $g,h$ theta series of ring class characters $\nu_g,\nu_h$ of $K$ with inverse central characters (Assumption 1.1 of loc. cit.), $\varphi=\nu_g\nu_h$, $\psi=\nu_g\nu_h^c$: $\log_{\beta\beta}\mathrm{res}_p\kappa_{\alpha\alpha}(f,g,h)=Q\cdot\log_{\omega_f}(P^\epsilon_\varphi)\log_{\omega_f}(P^\epsilon_\psi)$ with $Q\in\overline{\mathbb{Q}}^\times$ and $P^\epsilon_\bullet$ Heegner (resp. Stark–Heegner) points; for $K$ imaginary quadratic, $L''(f\otimes g\otimes h,1)\neq0\iff \kappa_{\alpha\alpha}\neq0$, and then $\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V(f,g,h))=L\kappa_{\alpha\alpha}\oplus L\kappa_{\beta\beta}$. This is the rank $(1,1)$ setting; the trivial representation appears only if $\varphi$ or $\psi$ is trivial, in which case $L(A/K,s)=L(A,s)L(A^K,s)$ has a *simple* zero, so $A(\mathbb{Q})$ has rank $\le 1$: this theorem never concerns rank two over $\mathbb{Q}$.

## 2. The rank-two paper of Darmon–Rotger, the elliptic Stark conjecture, and their status

### 2.1 Exact content of [DR16] = "Elliptic curves of rank two and generalised Kato classes", Res. Math. Sci. 3 (2016), Art. 27

Standing hypotheses [DR16, §2 (I)–(IV), §3.1]: $p$ splits completely in $L$; $V_g$ unramified at $p$; $g$ regular at $p$; **(IV)** $\varrho_g$ is not induced from a character of a real quadratic field in which $p$ splits; same for $h$; $\varrho_g,\varrho_h$ odd irreducible with $\det\varrho_g=\det\varrho_h^{-1}$; $p\nmid N$; the Artin conductor of $V_{gh}$ is prime to $N_f$ (so all local signs are $+1$ by Prasad).

`[THEOREM]` ([DR16, Thm. 3.1], restating [DR17, Thm. C] and [BDR15b, Thm. 3.10].) The image of $\kappa(f,g_\alpha,h_\alpha)$ in $H^1_{\mathrm{sing}}(\mathbb{Q}_p,V_{fgh})$ lies in $H^1_{\mathrm{sing}}(\mathbb{Q}_p,V_f\otimes V^{\beta\beta}_{gh})$ (and analogously for the other three classes), and the four classes $\kappa(f,g_\alpha,h_\alpha),\kappa(f,g_\alpha,h_\beta),\kappa(f,g_\beta,h_\alpha),\kappa(f,g_\beta,h_\beta)$ are Selmer for all choices of projection $\pi$ iff $L(E,V_{gh},1)=0$.

`[CONJECTURE]` ([DR16, Conj. 3.2].) Assume $L(E,V_{gh},1)=0$. The four classes generate a nontrivial subspace of $\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_{fgh})$ for a suitable $\pi$ iff (a) $L(E,V_{gh},s)$ has a double zero at $s=1$; (b) $(E(H)_L\otimes V_{12})^{G_\mathbb{Q}}$ is $2$-dimensional; (c) $\dim_{\mathbb{Q}_p}\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_{fgh})=2$ (the equivalence (a)$\iff$(b)$\iff$(c) being $\mathrm{BSD}(E,V_{gh})$). It is *not* predicted that all four classes are nonzero, nor that they generate the Selmer group [DR16, Rem. 3.3].

`[THEOREM]` ([DR16, Thm. 3.4], from [DR17, §6.4].) Write $\mathrm{res}_p\kappa(f,g_\alpha,h_\alpha)=R_{\alpha\alpha}\otimes v^{\beta\beta}_{gh}+R_{\alpha\beta}\otimes v^{\beta\alpha}_{gh}+R_{\beta\alpha}\otimes v^{\alpha\beta}_{gh}+R_{\beta\beta}\otimes v^{\alpha\alpha}_{gh}$ in $H^1_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathbb{Q}_p,V_{fgh})=(E(H_p)\otimes V_{gh})^{\mathrm{Gal}(H_p/\mathbb{Q}_p)}$, with $R_\xi\in E(H_p)^{\mathrm{Fr}_p=\xi}_{\mathbb{Q}_p}$, $\xi\in\{\alpha_g\alpha_h,\alpha_g\beta_h,\beta_g\alpha_h,\beta_g\beta_h\}$. If $L(E,V_{gh},1)=0$ then for suitable $\pi$ and test vectors
$$\log_p(R_{\alpha\beta})\sim\mathcal L_p^{g_\alpha}(\breve f,\breve g^*,\breve h),\qquad \log_p(R_{\beta\alpha})\sim\mathcal L_p^{h_\alpha}(\breve f,\breve g,\breve h^*),\qquad \log_p(R_{\beta\beta})=0 .$$
[DR16, Rem. 3.5]: "This theorem says nothing about $\log_p(R_{\alpha\alpha})$, which does not bear any direct relationship with the $p$-adic $L$-values introduced above. We expect that $\log_p(R_{\alpha\alpha})$ may rather be connected with the first derivative of a putative refinement of $\mathcal L_p^f(f,g_\alpha,h_\alpha)$ in which all three modular forms would be made to vary in a Hida family." This coordinate is exactly the one relevant to rank two over $\mathbb{Q}$ (§4.4).

`[CONJECTURE]` ([DR16, Conj. 3.8] $=$ [DLR15, Conj. ES] in the DR16 normalisation.) If $\operatorname{ord}_{s=1}L(E,V_{gh},s)=2$ then $\mathcal L_p^{g_\alpha}(\breve f,\breve g^*,\breve h)=\mathrm{Reg}_{g_\alpha}(E,V_{12})/\log_p u_{g_\alpha}\pmod{L^\times}$, where $\mathrm{Reg}_{g_\alpha}=\det\begin{pmatrix}\log_pP_{\beta\beta}&\log_pP_{\beta\alpha}\\ \log_pQ_{\beta\beta}&\log_pQ_{\beta\alpha}\end{pmatrix}$ is a $2\times2$ regulator of Frobenius-eigencomponents of a basis $P,Q$ of the two-dimensional $(E(H)_L\otimes V_{12})^{G_\mathbb{Q}}$, and $u_{g_\alpha}$ is the Gross–Stark unit in the $\mathrm{Ad}^0(V_g)$-isotypic part of $\mathcal O_H^\times$ on which $\mathrm{Fr}_p$ acts by $\beta_g/\alpha_g$ (well defined by (IV)).

`[CONJECTURE]` ([DR16, Conj. 3.12], the main new statement.) If $r(E,V_{gh})=2$ then $\kappa(f,g_\alpha,h_\alpha)\in(E(H)\otimes V_{gh})^{G_\mathbb{Q}}$ and $\omega_{g_\alpha}\omega_{h_\alpha}\otimes\kappa(f,g_\alpha,h_\alpha)\sim_L\widetilde{\mathrm{Reg}}_{\alpha\alpha}(E,V_{gh}):=\mathrm{Log}_{\alpha\alpha}(P)\otimes Q-\mathrm{Log}_{\alpha\alpha}(Q)\otimes P$ (the *enhanced regulator*). [DR16, Prop. 3.13]: Conj. 3.12 together with Conj. 2.1 ($\mathcal L_{g_\alpha}=\log_p u_{g_\alpha}$, a $p$-adic Stark conjecture for $\mathrm{Ad}^0 g$) implies Conj. 3.8.

**The adjoint case [DR16, §4.5].** Take $h=g^*$ (so $\alpha_h=\alpha_g^{-1},\beta_h=\beta_g^{-1}$, $\alpha_g\alpha_h=\beta_g\beta_h=1$, $\alpha_g\beta_h=\alpha_g/\beta_g$, $\beta_g\alpha_h=\beta_g/\alpha_g$). Then $V_{gh}=\mathbb{Q}_p\oplus M_g$, $M_g=\mathrm{Ad}^0(V_g)$, and $L(E,V_{gh},s)=L(E,s)L(E,M_g,s)$. Three "double zero" scenarios: rank $(0,2)$, $(1,1)$, $(2,0)$. In the **rank $(2,0)$ setting** ($L(E,s)$ has a double zero, $L(E,M_g,1)\neq0$), with $(P,Q)$ an $L$-basis of $E(\mathbb{Q})_L$ [DR16, §4.5.3]:
$$\widetilde{\mathrm{Reg}}_{\alpha\alpha}(E,V_{12})=\log_p(P)\cdot Q-\log_p(Q)\cdot P,\quad \widetilde{\mathrm{Reg}}_{\alpha\beta}=\widetilde{\mathrm{Reg}}_{\beta\alpha}=0,\quad \widetilde{\mathrm{Reg}}_{\beta\beta}=\log_p(P)\cdot Q-\log_p(Q)\cdot P,$$
and $\mathrm{Reg}_{g_\alpha}=\mathrm{Reg}_{g_\beta}=\mathrm{Reg}_{h_\alpha}=\mathrm{Reg}_{h_\beta}=0$. Quoting [DR16, p. 31]: "the generalised Kato classes $\kappa(f,g_\alpha,h_\alpha)$ and $\kappa(f,g_\beta,h_\beta)$ give (essentially the same, up to $L^\times$-multiples) canonical element of $E(\mathbb{Q})_{\mathbb{Q}_p}$, which is expected to be non-trivial precisely when $L''(E,1)\neq0$, $L(E,M_g,1)\neq0$ … Testing this prediction experimentally seems to present an interesting challenge." Note that the predicted element $\log_p(P)Q-\log_p(Q)P$ is precisely the generator of $\ker(\log_p)\subset E(\mathbb{Q})\otimes\mathbb{Q}_p$: the conjecture predicts a class that is **locally trivial at $p$**.

### 2.2 The elliptic Stark conjecture [DLR15] and its status

`[CONJECTURE]` ([DLR15, Conj. ES].) Hypotheses: **A** (local signs $\epsilon_v(E,\varrho_{gh})=+1$ for $v\mid N$; automatic if $\gcd(N_f,N_g,N_h)=1$), **B** ($L(E,\varrho_{gh},1)=0$), **C/C′** (classicality of the generalised eigenspace of $g_\alpha$: $g$ cuspidal regular at $p$ and not RM with $p$ split, or $g$ Eisenstein irregular at $p$), $\operatorname{ord}_p(N_f)\le1$, $p\nmid N_gN_h$. If $r(E,\varrho_{gh})>2$ the $p$-adic iterated integrals $\int_{\breve\gamma}\breve f\cdot\breve h$ vanish identically; if $r(E,\varrho_{gh})=2$ there are test vectors with $\int_{\breve\gamma}\breve f\cdot\breve h=\det R_{g_\alpha}(E,\varrho_{gh})/\log_p(u_{g_\alpha})$.

`[THEOREM]` ([DLR15, Thm. 3.3].) $N$ square-free, $p\nmid 2N$, Hypotheses C, C′, $g,h$ theta series of characters $\psi_g,\psi_h$ of the same imaginary quadratic $K$ in which $p$ splits, Heegner hypothesis, $V_{gh}=V_{\psi_1}\oplus V_{\psi_2}$: if $r_{\mathrm{an}}(E,V_{\psi_1})>1$ or $r_{\mathrm{an}}(E,V_{\psi_2})>1$ the iterated integrals vanish identically; otherwise $\int_{\breve\gamma_{g_\alpha}}\breve f\cdot\breve h=\lambda\log_{E,p}(P_1)\log_{E,p}(P_2)/\log_p(u_{g_\alpha})$, $\lambda\in L^\times$. Proof via the BDP $p$-adic Gross–Zagier formula and factorisations into Katz $p$-adic $L$-functions. In the rank $(2,0)$ adjoint CM setting this gives the *vanishing* of all four iterated integrals (cf. [DR16, §4.5.3]).

`[THEOREM]` (Rivero–Rotger [RR21], arXiv:1806.10022.) Proof of the DLR conjecture on iterated integrals and of the Darmon–Rotger conjecture for Beilinson–Flach elements in the *adjoint* setting at weights $(1,1,0)$ (units, not points), by "improved Euler systems" and Galois deformation theory, without CM. This is the only non-CM case of an elliptic-Stark-type statement proved to date that we found; it concerns $\mathrm{Ad}^0 g$ and units, not $E$.

`[THEOREM]` (Rivero [Riv21], arXiv:2103.00987, Indiana Univ. Math. J. to appear.) In the split multiplicative case for $E$ at $p$, the DLR conjecture is reduced, via $\mathcal L$-invariants of improved $p$-adic $L$-functions and *derived* diagonal classes, to an exceptional-zero statement about higher derivatives of a triple product $p$-adic $L$-function at a point inside the interpolation region.

### 2.3 Castella–Hsieh (2022) and Castella (2023, 2026): what is actually proved in rank two

Setting of [CH22]: $E/\mathbb{Q}$, $p>3$ good ordinary, $L(E,1)=0$ with root number $+1$; $K$ imaginary quadratic of discriminant prime to $N$, $p=\mathfrak p\bar{\mathfrak p}$ split; $\psi$ a ray class character of $K$ of conductor prime to $p$, $g=\theta_\psi$, $g^*=\theta_{\psi^{-1}}$, $\chi:=\psi/\psi^c$ (a ring class character), so that
$$V_g\otimes V_{g^*}=\mathrm{Ind}^\mathbb{Q}_K\mathbf 1\oplus\mathrm{Ind}^\mathbb{Q}_K\chi=\mathbf 1\oplus\varepsilon_K\oplus\mathrm{Ind}^\mathbb{Q}_K\chi,\qquad L(E,\mathrm{ad}^0 g,s)=L(E^K,s)\,L(E/K,\chi,s).$$
Hypotheses: (a) $L(E^K,1)L(E/K,\chi,1)\neq0$; (b) $\alpha\neq\beta$, i.e. $\chi(\mathfrak p)\neq1$; (c) $E[p]$ irreducible; (d) $N^-$ (the part of $N$ divisible only by primes inert in $K$) is a square-free product of an odd number of primes; (e) $E[p]$ ramified at every $q\mid N^-$. The four classes $\kappa_{\alpha,\alpha^{-1}},\kappa_{\alpha,\beta^{-1}},\kappa_{\beta,\alpha^{-1}},\kappa_{\beta,\beta^{-1}}(f,g,g^*)$ project to $\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)$ [CH22, Lemma 5.1].

`[THEOREM]` ([CH22, Thm. A].) $\kappa_{\alpha,\beta^{-1}}=\kappa_{\beta,\alpha^{-1}}=0$ (Prop. 5.2, via Cornut–Vatsal [CV05]: no anticyclotomic universal norms). Moreover
$$\kappa_{\alpha,\alpha^{-1}}(f,g,g^*)\neq0\ \Longrightarrow\ \dim_{\mathbb{Q}_p}\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)=2,\qquad\quad \dim_{\mathbb{Q}_p}\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)=2\ \&\ \mathrm{Sel}\neq\ker(\mathrm{Loc}_p)\ \Longrightarrow\ \kappa_{\alpha,\alpha^{-1}}(f,g,g^*)\neq0.$$
[CH22, Rem. 1.6]: $\kappa_{\alpha,\alpha^{-1}}$ and $\kappa_{\beta,\beta^{-1}}$ agree up to a nonzero algebraic constant and span the line $\ker(\log_p)\subset\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)$; when Ш$[p^\infty]$ is finite this is the line of $\mathrm{Log}_p(P\wedge Q)=P\otimes\log_p(Q)-Q\otimes\log_p(P)$.

`[THEOREM]` ([CH22, Thm. 5.3, Thm. B].) Let $\Theta_{f/K}\in\mathbb{Z}_p[[\Gamma^-_\infty]]$ be the Bertolini–Darmon anticyclotomic theta element [BD96] and $\rho:=\operatorname{ord}_J\Theta_{f/K}$ ($J$ the augmentation ideal; $\rho\ge1$ since $L(E/K,1)=0$). Then $\kappa_{\alpha,\alpha^{-1}}\in S^{(\rho)}$ (Howard's derived-height filtration [How04], extended to square-free $N^-$ in [CH22, Thm. 4.1]) and for all $x\in S^{(\rho)}$
$$h^{(\rho)}\big(\kappa_{\alpha,\alpha^{-1}}(f,g,g^*),x\big)=C\cdot\frac{1-\alpha_p^{-1}}{1-p\alpha_p^{-1}}\cdot\Theta_{f/K}\cdot\log_{\omega,p}(x)\pmod{J^{\rho+1}},$$
$C$ a nonzero algebraic number. Consequently (Thm. B): if in addition $\operatorname{rank}E(\mathbb{Q})>0$ and $\operatorname{ord}_J\Theta_{f/K}=2$ then $\kappa_{\alpha,\alpha^{-1}}\neq0$. The Appendix verifies $\operatorname{ord}_J\Theta_{f/K}=2$ numerically (Brandt modules, Sage) for $21$ curves of rank $2$, e.g. $389a1$ ($p=11$), $433a1$, $446c1$, $563a1$, $571b1$, $643a1$, $709a1$, $997b1$, $997c1$, $1621a1$: the first examples of nonzero generalised Kato classes in rank two.

`[THEOREM]` ([CH22, Thm. 5.5].) As elements of $\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)\simeq E(\mathbb{Q})\otimes\mathbb{Q}_p$ (Ш$[p^\infty]$ finite): $\kappa_{\alpha,\alpha^{-1}}=C\cdot\frac{1-\alpha_p^{-1}}{1-p\alpha_p^{-1}}\cdot\frac{\Theta^{(\rho)}_{f/K}}{h^{(\rho)}(P,Q)}\cdot\mathrm{Log}_p(P\wedge Q)$; the algebraicity of the ratio predicted by [DR16, Conj. 3.12] is thereby reduced to a refinement of the anticyclotomic $p$-adic BSD conjecture [BD96, Conj. 4.3] for derived heights.

`[THEOREM]` (Castella [Cas23, Thm. 5.2.3], arXiv:2312.01481.) Hypotheses: $p>3$ good ordinary; (h1) $\bar\rho_f$ absolutely irreducible; (h2) there is $q\,\|\,N_f$, and if $q\equiv\pm1\pmod p$ then $\bar\rho_f$ is ramified at $q$; (h3) $p>5$ or the image of $\bar\rho_f$ contains a conjugate of $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{F}_p)$; $(K,\chi)$ chosen as in [Cas23, Choice 5.2.1] ($q$ inert in $K$, all other primes of $N_f$ split, $\chi$ of $\ell$-power conductor with $\ell$ split and ordinary, $L(E^K,1)L(E/K,\chi,1)\neq0$, $\chi|_{G_{K_\mathfrak p}}\neq1$; existence by [BFH90], [Vat03]). Suppose $L(E,s)$ vanishes to positive even order at $s=1$ and let $\kappa_p(E)$ be the resulting generalised Kato class. Then
$$\kappa_p(E)\neq0\ \Longrightarrow\ \dim_{\mathbb{Q}_p}\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)=2;$$
conversely, if $\dim_{\mathbb{Q}_p}\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)=2$ then $\kappa_p(E)\neq0$ **if and only if** $\mathrm{loc}_p:\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)\to E(\mathbb{Q}_p)\hat\otimes\mathbb{Q}_p$ is nonzero, and in that case $\kappa_p(E)$ spans $\mathrm{Sel}_0(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)=\ker(\mathrm{loc}_p)$. The proof compares two formulations of the Iwasawa–Greenberg main conjecture for triple products ([ACR23]: one via Hsieh's $p$-adic $L$-function, one via the family of diagonal classes), reduces to the anticyclotomic main conjecture for $E/K$ (Bertolini–Darmon [BD05], Skinner–Urban [SU14]), and uses the $p$-parity theorem [Nek01]. [Cas23, Rem. 5.2.5]: when $\dim\mathrm{Sel}=2$ and $\mathrm{Sel}\neq\ker(\mathrm{loc}_p)$, $\kappa_p(E)=\lambda(\log_p(Q)P-\log_p(P)Q)$ for a basis $(P,Q)$ of $\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)$ and $\lambda\in\mathbb{Q}_p^\times$. [Cas23, Rem. 5.1.4]: when $L(f/K,1)=0$ the class lies in the kernel of restriction at *both* primes above $p$.

`[THEOREM]` (Castella [Cas26], arXiv:2204.09608v3, to appear Amer. J. Math.) For $E/\mathbb{Q}$ **with CM** by $K$, $p\ge5$ split in $K$, sign $+1$, $L(E,1)=0$: a twisted variant $\kappa_p\in\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)$ (built from three CM Hida families with $\psi_E=\lambda_0\lambda_1^c\lambda_2^c$ and three auxiliary nonvanishing Hecke $L$-values, [Cas26, (1.5)]) satisfies the same equivalence as above (Thm. B), and (Thm. C) if Ш$[p^\infty]$ is finite and the Katz $p$-adic $L$-functions satisfy $\operatorname{ord}_{s=1}L_{\mathfrak p}(s)=2$, $\operatorname{ord}_{s=1}L^*_{\mathfrak p}(s)=1$, then $\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)=\mathbb{Q}_p\kappa_p\oplus\mathbb{Q}_p x^{(2)}_{\mathfrak p}$ with $x^{(2)}_{\mathfrak p}$ a *derived* elliptic unit (Rubin).

## 3. Beilinson–Flach elements and the structure of the rank-two case there

`[THEOREM]` (Lei–Loeffler–Zerbes [LLZ14]; Kings–Loeffler–Zerbes [KLZ17, KLZ20]; Bertolini–Darmon–Rotger [BDR15a, BDR15b].) Rankin–Eisenstein/Beilinson–Flach classes for $f\otimes g$ form an Euler system (norm-compatible in the cyclotomic tower and in the tame direction) with an explicit reciprocity law computing their image under the Perrin-Riou regulator as the Rankin–Selberg $p$-adic $L$-function. `[THEOREM]` ([KLZ17, Thm. 11.7.4].) Let $E/\mathbb{Q}$ be non-CM, $\rho$ a $2$-dimensional odd irreducible Artin representation with splitting field $F$, $\mathfrak P\mid p$ a prime of the coefficient field, and assume: (i) $\gcd(N_E,N_\rho)=1$; (ii) $p\ge5$; (iii) $p\nmid N_EN_\rho$; (iv) $G_\mathbb{Q}\to\mathrm{Aut}_{\mathbb{Z}_p}(T_pE)$ surjective; (v) $E$ ordinary at $p$; (vi) $\rho(\mathrm{Frob}_p)$ has distinct eigenvalues mod $\mathfrak P$. If $L(E,\rho,1)\neq0$ then $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}_p[\mathrm{Gal}(F/\mathbb{Q})]}(\rho,\mathrm{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/F))$ is finite (so both $E(F)^\rho$ and Ш$_{p^\infty}(E/F)^\rho$ are finite). The Mordell–Weil part was proved earlier by [BDR15b] by the Darmon–Rotger-type argument; the Euler-system argument is what gives Ш. (The Nov 2023 correction appended to arXiv:1503.02888 concerns the definition of "cuspidal branch" of a Hida family and does not affect this theorem.)

**Rank one.** In the Beilinson–Flach setting the "derived" phenomena are: (i) the $(1,1,0)$ adjoint setting of Rivero–Rotger [RR21] (units); (ii) the Perrin-Riou-type comparison of Beilinson–Kato/Beilinson–Flach classes with Heegner points in rank one (Bertolini–Darmon–Venerucci [BDV22]; Alonso–Omil-Pazos–Rivero [AOR25] for the anticyclotomic diagonal-cycle system). In all these, the trivial representation is *not* the relevant constituent.

**Rank two.** In [DR16, §4.2] the Beilinson–Flach case is $h=E_1(\mathbf 1,\chi^{-1})$, so $V_{gh}=V_g\oplus V_{\bar g}$ and the double-zero regime is the rank $(1,1)$ setting: two rank-one pieces, $\widetilde{\mathrm{Reg}}_{\alpha\alpha}=\log_p(\bar P_{\alpha_g^{-1}})\cdot P$ etc. The following elementary lemma shows that this is forced.

**Lemma 3.1** `[NEW]` (elementary; multiplicity of the trivial representation in the Garrett–Rankin–Selberg family). Let $V_g,V_h$ be two-dimensional Artin representations with $\det V_g\det V_h=\mathbf 1$.
(a) If $V_g$ is irreducible and $V_h=\chi_1\oplus\chi_2$ is reducible, then $\mathbf 1\not\subset V_g\otimes V_h$.
(b) If both are reducible, $V_g=\chi_1\oplus\chi_2$, $V_h=\psi_1\oplus\psi_2$, then $\mathbf 1$ occurs in $V_g\otimes V_h$ with even multiplicity ($0$, $2$ or $4$).
(c) If both are irreducible, $\mathbf 1\subset V_g\otimes V_h$ iff $V_h\simeq V_g^\vee$, and then the multiplicity is exactly one.

*Proof.* (a) $V_g\otimes V_h=V_g\otimes\chi_1\oplus V_g\otimes\chi_2$, each summand irreducible of dimension $2$. (b) The constituents are $\chi_i\psi_j$; if $\chi_1\psi_1=\mathbf 1$ then $\chi_2\psi_2=(\chi_1\chi_2\psi_1\psi_2)(\chi_1\psi_1)^{-1}=\mathbf 1$; similarly $\chi_1\psi_2=\mathbf1\Rightarrow\chi_2\psi_1=\mathbf 1$; so trivial constituents come in pairs. (c) $\operatorname{Hom}_{G}(\mathbf 1,V_g\otimes V_h)=\operatorname{Hom}_G(V_h^\vee,V_g)$, which is $0$ unless $V_h^\vee\simeq V_g$, and then one-dimensional by Schur. $\square$

**Corollary 3.2.** In the Beilinson–Kato case (b) with the trivial constituent present, $L(E,V_{gh},s)=L(E,s)^2L(E,\chi_1\psi_2,s)L(E,\chi_2\psi_1,s)$; a double zero at $s=1$ with the trivial part contributing forces $r_{\mathrm{an}}(E)=1$ — Perrin-Riou's rank-one setting, in which the Beilinson–Kato class is $\sim\log_p(P)\cdot P$ ([DR16, §4.1]; [BDV22]). For $r_{\mathrm{an}}(E)=2$ the triple-product $L$-function vanishes to order $\ge4$ and the class is expected to be zero ([DR16, abstract]: "expected to be trivial when $\operatorname{ord}_{s=1}L(E,V_{gh},s)>2$"). Hence among Kato, Beilinson–Flach and diagonal-cycle classes, **only** the cuspidal adjoint case $h=g^*$ of §4 can produce a class in $\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)$ in a rank-two regime for $E$.

## 4. The crux: rank two over $\mathbb{Q}$ and the trivial representation ($h=g^*$)

### 4.1 Factorisation, shifts, signs

Fix $g\in S_1(N_g,\chi)$ cuspidal, and put $h:=g^*=g\otimes\chi^{-1}\in S_1(N_g,\chi^{-1})$. Since $\det V_g=\chi$, $V_{g^*}=V_g\otimes\chi^{-1}=V_g^\vee$ (no Tate twist in weight one), and the trace pairing gives the canonical $G_\mathbb{Q}$-decomposition
$$V_{gh}=V_g\otimes V_g^\vee=\operatorname{End}(V_g)=\mathbb{Q}_p\cdot\mathrm{id}\ \oplus\ \mathrm{Ad}^0(V_g),\qquad \mathrm{pr}_{\mathbf 1}:=\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}:V_{gh}\to\mathbb{Q}_p .$$
`[THEOREM]` (Artin formalism; [DR16, §4.5]; [CH22, (1.7)].) $L(f\otimes g\otimes g^*,s)=L(E,V_{gh},s)=L(E,s)\cdot L(E,\mathrm{Ad}^0 V_g,s)$, all three centred at $s=1$. (For general weights $(k,\ell,\ell)$, $V_{g_\ell}\otimes V_{g_\ell^*}$ is a Tate twist of $\mathbb{Q}_p\oplus\mathrm{Ad}^0(V_{g_\ell})$, the Rankin–Selberg $L(g_\ell\otimes g_\ell^*,s)$ has its pole at $s=\ell$, and $L(f_k\otimes g_\ell\otimes g_\ell^*,s)=L(f_k,s-\ell+1)\,L(f_k\otimes\mathrm{Ad}^0g_\ell,s-\ell+1)$ with centre $c=k/2+\ell-1$, so that the first factor at $s=c$ is the central value $L(f_k,k/2)$.)

**Lemma 4.1** (signs; elementary). Assume $\gcd(N_f,N_g)=1$. Then $\varepsilon(E)\cdot\varepsilon(E,\mathrm{Ad}^0V_g)=+1$. In particular if $r_{\mathrm{an}}(E)$ is even then $\varepsilon(E,\mathrm{Ad}^0V_g)=+1$ and $\operatorname{ord}_{s=1}L(E,\mathrm{Ad}^0V_g,s)$ is even; the archimedean root number of $E\otimes\mathrm{Ad}^0V_g$ is $-1$.
*Proof.* Global root numbers are multiplicative in direct sums, and $\varepsilon(E,V_{gh})=+1$ by Prasad's theorem under the coprimality ([DR16, §3.1]). For the archimedean sign: $V_g|_{W_\mathbb{R}}=\mathbf 1\oplus\mathrm{sgn}$ (odd), so $\mathrm{Ad}^0V_g|_{W_\mathbb{R}}=\mathbf 1\oplus\mathrm{sgn}^{\oplus2}$; $V_f|_{W_\mathbb{R}}$ is induced from $\mathbb{C}^\times$, hence $V_f\otimes\mathrm{sgn}\simeq V_f$ as $W_\mathbb{R}$-representations, and $\varepsilon_\infty(E\otimes\mathrm{Ad}^0V_g)=\varepsilon_\infty(E)^3=(-1)^3=-1$. $\square$

So for $E$ of analytic rank $2$ the natural target is the **rank $(2,0)$ setting**: $r_{\mathrm{an}}(E)=2$ and $L(E,\mathrm{Ad}^0V_g,1)\neq0$ — then $\operatorname{ord}_{s=1}L(f\otimes g\otimes g^*,s)=2$ exactly, the "double zero" regime of [DR16, Conj. 3.2]. The other two settings put the double zero in $\mathrm{Ad}^0$ and say nothing about $E(\mathbb{Q})$.

### 4.2 Interpolation regions on the adjoint plane

Let $\mathbf g$ be the Hida family through $g_\alpha$ and $\mathbf g^*$ its dual family (through $g^*_{\alpha^{-1}}$), with weights $(k,\ell,m)$ on $\mathbf f,\mathbf g,\mathbf g^*$. The *adjoint plane* is $\{m=\ell\}$; on it $V_{\mathbf g_\ell}\otimes V_{\mathbf g^*_\ell}\supset\mathbb{Q}_p(\ell-1)$ for every $\ell$.

* $f$-dominant $\mathcal L_p^f(\mathbf f,\mathbf g,\mathbf g^*)$: interpolation region $k\ge\ell+m=2\ell$. The whole line $(k,1,1)$, $k\ge2$ even, consists of interpolation points, and by Hsieh's interpolation formula ([Hsi21, Thm. A], as stated in [CH22, Thm. 2.1] under (CR), (ev), (sq), $\Sigma^-=\emptyset$)
$$\mathcal L_p^f(k,1,1)^2=\Gamma(k,1,1)\,\mathcal E_p(f_k,g,g^*)^2\,\frac{L(f_k,k/2)\,L(f_k\otimes\mathrm{Ad}^0g,k/2)}{\Omega_{f_k}^2}\cdot(\text{explicit nonzero factors}),$$
with $\mathcal E_p(f,g,g^*)=(1-\beta_f p^{-1})^2(1-\beta_f\tfrac{\alpha_g}{\beta_g}p^{-1})(1-\beta_f\tfrac{\beta_g}{\alpha_g}p^{-1})$ at $(2,1,1)$, which is **nonzero** since $|\beta_f|=p^{1/2}$ and $|\alpha_g/\beta_g|=1$. There is no exceptional zero for good $p$ (consistent with [BSV22a, §1.2]: exceptional zeros at $(2,1,1)$ require $p\,\|\,c(f_2)$). Hence, by [DR17, Thm. C]/[BSV22a, Thm. B], $\mathcal L^f_p(2,1,1)=0\iff L(E,1)L(E,\mathrm{Ad}^0V_g,1)=0$, and in the rank $(2,0)$ setting the generalised Kato classes are Selmer.
* $g$-dominant $\mathcal L_p^g(\mathbf f,\mathbf g,\mathbf g^*)$: interpolation region $\ell\ge k+m=k+\ell$, i.e. $k\le0$: **empty on the adjoint plane**. The elliptic Stark value $\mathcal L_p^{g_\alpha}(\breve f,\breve g^*,\breve g)$ at $(2,1,1)$ is a value of a $p$-adic $L$-function nowhere interpolating classical values along the plane in which the degeneration $h=g^*$ lives. This is the precise sense in which "$h=g^*$ makes the interpolation region degenerate". Same for $\mathcal L_p^h$.

### 4.3 Which component of $\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_{fgh})=\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)\oplus\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE\otimes\mathrm{Ad}^0V_g)$ the class lands in

Put $\kappa_p(E):=\mathrm{pr}_{\mathbf 1}\,\kappa(f,g_\alpha,g^*_{\alpha^{-1}})\in H^1(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)$, and $\kappa^{\mathrm{ad}}:=(1-\mathrm{pr}_{\mathbf 1})\kappa\in H^1(\mathbb{Q},V_pE\otimes\mathrm{Ad}^0V_g)$.

`[THEOREM]` (from [DR17, Thm. C], [BSV22a, Thm. B], [CH22, Lemma 5.1], [Cas23, Cor. 5.1.3].) If $L(E,1)=0$ then $\kappa_p(E)\in\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)$ and $\kappa^{\mathrm{ad}}\in\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE\otimes\mathrm{Ad}^0V_g)$. In the rank $(2,0)$ setting with $g$ CM and $L(E^K,1)L(E/K,\chi,1)\neq0$, $\kappa^{\mathrm{ad}}=0$ because $\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE^K)=0=\mathrm{Sel}(K,V_pE\otimes\chi)$ by Kolyvagin/Kato and Chida–Hsieh ([CH22, Lemma 3.5, Lemma 5.1]). For non-CM $g$ the vanishing of $\kappa^{\mathrm{ad}}$ would follow from $\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE\otimes\mathrm{Ad}^0V_g)=0$, which is the Bloch–Kato prediction for $L(E,\mathrm{Ad}^0V_g,1)\neq0$ but is not known (`[GAP C.5]`).

### 4.4 The local behaviour of $\kappa_p(E)$ at $p$: exactly what is and is not controlled

Locally at $p$ decompose $V_f=V_f^+\oplus V_f^-$ ($V_f^+$ the kernel of reduction, Hodge–Tate weight $1$; $V_f^-$ unramified with $\mathrm{Fr}_p=\alpha_f$) and $V_g=V_g^{\alpha}\oplus V_g^{\beta}$, $V_{g^*}=V_{g^*}^{\alpha^{-1}}\oplus V_{g^*}^{\beta^{-1}}$ into Frobenius eigenlines. For the $p$-stabilisation $g_\alpha$ the Hida-family filtration has sub $U_g=V_g^{\beta}$ and unramified quotient $W_g=V_g^{\alpha}$ ([DR16, §2 (b′)]); for $g^*_{\alpha^{-1}}$ the sub is $V_{g^*}^{\beta^{-1}}$. By [DR22a, Prop. 5.8] / [BSV22a, §7.2] (as used in [CH22, proof of Thm. 3.6]) $\mathrm{res}_p\kappa(f,g_\alpha,g^*_{\alpha^{-1}})$ lies in the image of $H^1(\mathbb{Q}_p,\mathcal F^{++})$, where $\mathcal F^{++}$ is the rank-four "balanced" subspace spanned by the eight-fold eigenline decomposition pieces with at least two "sub" factors:
$$\mathcal F^{++}=V_f^+V_g^\beta V_{g^*}^{\beta^{-1}}\ \oplus\ V_f^+V_g^\beta V_{g^*}^{\alpha^{-1}}\ \oplus\ V_f^+V_g^\alpha V_{g^*}^{\beta^{-1}}\ \oplus\ V_f^-V_g^\beta V_{g^*}^{\beta^{-1}} .$$
The trace $\mathrm{pr}_{\mathbf 1}$ is nonzero only on the eigenvalue-one lines $V_g^\alpha V_{g^*}^{\alpha^{-1}}$ and $V_g^\beta V_{g^*}^{\beta^{-1}}$ (Frobenius equivariance). Hence:

**Lemma 4.3** (bookkeeping; a direct reading of [DR16, Thm. 3.4] in the adjoint case). Write, as in [DR16, (24)], $\mathrm{res}_p\kappa(f,g_\alpha,g^*_{\alpha^{-1}})=R_{\alpha\alpha}\otimes v^{\beta\beta}+R_{\alpha\beta}\otimes v^{\beta\alpha}+R_{\beta\alpha}\otimes v^{\alpha\beta}+R_{\beta\beta}\otimes v^{\alpha\alpha}$; in the adjoint case $R_{\alpha\alpha},R_{\beta\beta}\in E(\mathbb{Q}_p)\otimes\mathbb{Q}_p$ (eigenvalue $\alpha_g\alpha_h=\beta_g\beta_h=1$) and $R_{\alpha\beta},R_{\beta\alpha}$ lie in the $\mathrm{Ad}^0$-components. Then
(i) the singular part of $\kappa_p(E)$ at $p$ is the $V_f^-V_g^\beta V_{g^*}^{\beta^{-1}}$-component, computed by $\mathcal L_p^f(f,g_\alpha,g^*_{\alpha^{-1}})(2,1,1)$; it vanishes in the rank $(2,0)$ setting (§4.2);
(ii) $\mathrm{pr}_{\mathbf 1}(\mathrm{res}_p\kappa)=c_1R_{\alpha\alpha}+c_2R_{\beta\beta}$ with $c_1,c_2\in\mathbb{Q}_p^\times$ the values of $\mathrm{pr}_{\mathbf 1}$ on $v^{\beta\beta},v^{\alpha\alpha}$, and $R_{\beta\beta}=0$ by [DR16, Thm. 3.4] (its logarithm vanishes and $\log_p$ is injective on $E(\mathbb{Q}_p)\otimes\mathbb{Q}_p$); therefore
$$\mathrm{loc}_p\,\kappa_p(E)=c_1R_{\alpha\alpha}\in H^1_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathbb{Q}_p,V_pE)=E(\mathbb{Q}_p)\hat\otimes\mathbb{Q}_p ,$$
the one coordinate about which [DR16, Rem. 3.5] says the four $p$-adic $L$-values carry no information;
(iii) for the mixed stabilisations $(g_\alpha,g^*_{\beta^{-1}})$ the corresponding $\mathcal F^{++}$ meets the eigenvalue-one lines only inside $V_f^+\otimes(\cdot)$, so $\mathrm{pr}_{\mathbf 1}\kappa(f,g_\alpha,g^*_{\beta^{-1}})$ is *automatically* crystalline at $p$, independently of any $L$-value; its vanishing (proved for CM $g$ in [CH22, Prop. 5.2]) is a global statement.
*Proof.* (i)–(iii) are immediate from the description of $\mathcal F^{++}$, the Frobenius-equivariance of $\mathrm{pr}_{\mathbf 1}$, [DR16, Thm. 3.1, Thm. 3.4], and the fact that $\mathrm{Fr}_p$ generates $\mathrm{Gal}(H_p/\mathbb{Q}_p)$ so that $E(H_p)^{\mathrm{Fr}_p=1}=E(\mathbb{Q}_p)$. $\square$

What is known about $R_{\alpha\alpha}$:

* `[THEOREM]` (CM $g$; [CH22, Thm. 3.6, Rem. 1.6], [Cas23, Rem. 5.1.4, Thm. 5.2.3].) $R_{\alpha\alpha}=0$: the anticyclotomic $\Lambda$-adic class $\kappa_\infty$ satisfies $\mathrm{Loc}_{\bar{\mathfrak p}}(\kappa_\infty)=0$ because $\mathcal F^{++}$ contains the full $V_f\otimes\Psi^{1-c}_{\mathfrak p}$ but nothing at $\bar{\mathfrak p}$; since a class in $H^1(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)=H^1(K,V_pE)^+$ has conjugate localisations at $\mathfrak p,\bar{\mathfrak p}$, the bottom class satisfies $\mathrm{loc}_p\kappa_p(E)=0$: $\kappa_p(E)\in\mathrm{Sel}_0(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)$. The anticyclotomic deformation breaks the $\mathfrak p\leftrightarrow\bar{\mathfrak p}$ symmetry; this is the mechanism, and it has no analogue for non-CM $g$.
* `[CONJECTURE]` (non-CM $g$.) [DR16, Conj. 3.12] predicts $\kappa_p(E)\sim\log_p(P)Q-\log_p(Q)P$, whose localisation is $\log_p(P)\log_p(Q)-\log_p(Q)\log_p(P)=0$; i.e. $R_{\alpha\alpha}=0$ always. This is `[GAP C.3]`.
* `[HEURISTIC]` ([DR16, Rem. 3.5].) $\log_p(R_{\alpha\alpha})$ should be governed by $\partial_k\mathcal L_p^f(\mathbf f,g_\alpha,g^*_{\alpha^{-1}})(k,1,1)|_{k=2}$ (the derivative in the weight of $f$ of the restriction to the line through $(2,1,1)$ of the $f$-dominant function, which vanishes at $k=2$). If a "derivative of the reciprocity law" of the kind proved in [BSV22a, §6], [BSV22b, Prop. 2.2] (second derivative along $(k,1,1)$ in the exceptional case) were available at this non-exceptional point, then the CM theorem $R_{\alpha\alpha}=0$ would force $\partial_k\mathcal L_p^f(k,1,1)|_{k=2}=0$ for CM $g$, i.e. $\mathcal L^f_p$ would vanish to order $\ge2$ along $(k,1,1)$, and by the interpolation formula the restriction $k\mapsto L(f_k,k/2)L(f_k\otimes\mathrm{Ad}^0g,k/2)/\Omega^2_{f_k}$ would have a zero of order $\ge4$ at $k=2$. We record this as a testable prediction, not a theorem; we found no factorisation of $\mathcal L^f_p(\mathbf f,g,g^*)$ along $(k,1,1)$ in the degenerate case in the literature ([BSV22b, §3] treats the non-degenerate CM case, [CH22, Prop. 2.5] the anticyclotomic line).

### 4.5 Is $\kappa_p(E)$ known to vanish or not in analytic rank $2$?

* For CM $g$ satisfying the hypotheses of [Cas23, Thm. 5.2.3] (which by [Cas23, Rem. 5.2.2] can be arranged for any $E$ with a prime $q\,\|\,N_f$ such that $\bar\rho_f$ is irreducible, (h2), (h3)): $\kappa_p(E)\neq0\iff\dim\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)=2$ and $\mathrm{Sel}\not\subset\ker\mathrm{loc}_p$. So `[THEOREM]`: in analytic rank $2$, $\kappa_p(E)\neq0$ **whenever** $\operatorname{rank}E(\mathbb{Q})=2$ and Ш$[p^\infty]$ is finite (then $\mathrm{Sel}=E(\mathbb{Q})\otimes\mathbb{Q}_p$ has dimension $2$ and $E(\mathbb{Q})\to E(\mathbb{Q}_p)$ is injective mod torsion), and $\kappa_p(E)=0$ whenever $\dim\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)\neq2$. There is no theorem deducing either alternative from $r_{\mathrm{an}}(E)=2$.
* For non-CM $g$: nothing beyond $\kappa_p(E)\in\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)$ is known; even the existence of an admissible $g$ with $L(E,\mathrm{Ad}^0V_g,1)\neq0$ is not a theorem (`[GAP C.4]`).
* The mixed classes $\kappa_{\alpha,\beta^{-1}},\kappa_{\beta,\alpha^{-1}}$ vanish for CM $g$ `[THEOREM]` [CH22, Prop. 5.2]; predicted to vanish in general `[CONJECTURE]` [DR16, §4.5.3].

### 4.6 The obstruction, stated precisely

**Proposition 4.9** `[NEW]` (formal corollary of [Cas23, Thm. 5.2.3] and [CH22, Rem. 1.5]). Let $E,p,(K,\chi)$ satisfy the hypotheses of [Cas23, Thm. 5.2.3], with $L(E,s)$ vanishing to positive even order at $s=1$, and let $\kappa_p(E)$ be the associated generalised Kato class. The following are equivalent:
(a) $\kappa_p(E)\neq0$;
(b) $\operatorname{corank}_{\mathbb{Z}_p}\mathrm{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/\mathbb{Q})=2$ and the image of $\mathrm{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/\mathbb{Q})$ in $H^1(\mathbb{Q}_p,E[p^\infty])$ is infinite;
(c) $\operatorname{corank}_{\mathbb{Z}_p}\mathrm{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/\mathbb{Q})=2$ and $\operatorname{rank}E(\mathbb{Q})\ge1$, **or** $\operatorname{corank}_{\mathbb{Z}_p}\mathrm{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/\mathbb{Q})=2$, $E(\mathbb{Q})$ finite and Ш$(E/\mathbb{Q})[p^\infty]$ has a divisible part not contained in the fine Selmer group.
If Ш$(E/\mathbb{Q})[p^\infty]$ is finite, all three are equivalent to $\operatorname{rank}E(\mathbb{Q})=2$.
Consequently: any argument establishing "$r_{\mathrm{an}}(E)=2\Rightarrow\kappa_p(E)\neq0$" establishes "$r_{\mathrm{an}}(E)=2\Rightarrow\operatorname{corank}\mathrm{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/\mathbb{Q})=2$", i.e. the $p^\infty$-Selmer form of BSD(rank) in analytic rank two.

*Proof.* (a)$\iff$(b) is [Cas23, Thm. 5.2.3] after noting that $\dim_{\mathbb{Q}_p}\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)=\operatorname{corank}\mathrm{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/\mathbb{Q})$ and that $\mathrm{loc}_p\neq0$ on $\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)$ iff the localisation of $\mathrm{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/\mathbb{Q})$ at $p$ is infinite. (b)$\iff$(c): if $E(\mathbb{Q})$ has a point of infinite order its image in $E(\mathbb{Q}_p)\hat\otimes\mathbb{Q}_p\subset H^1(\mathbb{Q}_p,V_pE)$ is nonzero; if $E(\mathbb{Q})$ is finite, $\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)=T_p$Ш$\otimes\mathbb{Q}_p$, and $\mathrm{loc}_p\neq0$ on it means exactly that the divisible part of Ш$[p^\infty]$ is not contained in the fine Selmer group. The last assertion is [CH22, Rem. 1.5]. $\square$

**Where the analytic information could enter.** The only proved analytic handle is the *anticyclotomic* leading-term formula [CH22, Thm. 5.3]: $h^{(\rho)}(\kappa_p(E),x)\doteq\Theta^{(\rho)}_{f/K}\log_p(x)$. Two remarks make the resulting gap precise.

(i) `[THEOREM]` (implicit in [CH22, §5.4].) Assume the hypotheses of [CH22, Thm. A] and $\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)\not\subset\ker\mathrm{loc}_p$. Since $L(E^K,1)\neq0$, $\mathrm{Sel}(K,V_pE)=\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)$ is fixed by complex conjugation $\tau$, and the anticyclotomic derived heights satisfy $h^{(i)}(\tau x,\tau y)=(-1)^ih^{(i)}(x,y)$ [CH22, Thm. 4.1(d)]; so $h^{(i)}\equiv0$ on $\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)$ for odd $i$. Feeding $\rho=1$ into the leading-term formula would give $\Theta^{(1)}_{f/K}\log_p(x)=0$ for all $x\in\mathrm{Sel}$, contradicting $\mathrm{Sel}\not\subset\ker\log_p$; hence $\rho=\operatorname{ord}_J\Theta_{f/K}\ge2$: the anticyclotomic $p$-adic $L$-function has a forced double zero at the trivial character. (Under $\dim\mathrm{Sel}=2$, [CH22, (5.8)] shows $\rho$ is even and $\operatorname{Sel}^\vee_{p^\infty}(E/K_\infty^-)\sim(\Lambda/J^{\rho})^{2}\oplus M$.)

(ii) `[GAP C.2]` The statement $\rho=2$ is equivalent (given $\dim\mathrm{Sel}=2$) to the nondegeneracy of the *second* derived anticyclotomic height $h^{(2)}$ on $\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)$ — an alternating form on a two-dimensional space, nondegenerate iff $h^{(2)}(P,Q)\neq0$. This is a $p$-adic transcendence-type statement (an anticyclotomic analogue of Schneider's nondegeneracy conjecture for the cyclotomic $p$-adic height), and it is not known to follow from $r_{\mathrm{an}}(E)=2$; no formula relates $\Theta^{(2)}_{f/K}$ to $L''(E,1)$ (the interpolation property of $\Theta_{f/K}$ only sees order zero: $\Theta_{f/K}(\zeta-1)^2\sim L(E/K,\chi_\zeta,1)$).

**Why no reciprocity law can help.** $\kappa_p(E)$ lies in $\mathrm{Sel}_0(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)=\ker(\mathrm{loc}_p)$ (`[THEOREM]` for CM $g$, `[CONJECTURE]` in general). Every Bloch–Kato local invariant at $p$ ($\exp^*$, $\log$, the Perrin-Riou regulator of any cyclotomic deformation of the class restricted to $\mathbb{Q}_p$) is a function of $\mathrm{loc}_p$ and therefore vanishes on $\kappa_p(E)$. A Gross–Zagier-type formula for $\kappa_p(E)$ must be a *global* height formula; the only one available is the anticyclotomic derived-height formula above. On the complex side, the natural regulator in BSD is $\hat h(P)\hat h(Q)-\langle P,Q\rangle^2$, whereas $\kappa_p(E)\sim\log_p(Q)P-\log_p(P)Q$ mixes $p$-adic logarithms with points; there is no known height pairing that evaluates to a real number on such an object, and the classes are not geometric (they are $p$-adic limits of weight-$(2,\ell,\ell)$ diagonal cycles, i.e. limits of Chow–Heegner classes [DRS12]), so the complex Beilinson–Bloch height of [GK92]/[YZZ23] is not defined for them.

### 4.7 Conclusion of §4

* `[THEOREM]` For $g$ CM and under explicit hypotheses, the diagonal-cycle construction yields a canonical class $\kappa_p(E)\in\mathrm{Sel}_0(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)$ with $\kappa_p(E)\neq0\iff(\dim\mathrm{Sel}=2$ and $\mathrm{Sel}\not\subset\ker\mathrm{loc}_p)$ [CH22, Cas23, Cas26]. Its nonvanishing is detected by the second derived anticyclotomic height against the leading term of $\Theta_{f/K}$, verified numerically for $21$ rank-two curves.
* `[CONJECTURE]` (Darmon–Rotger's own conjectures for the degenerate case.) [DR16, Conj. 3.2] specialised to §4.5.3 ($=$ [CH22, Conj. 1.2]): $\kappa_p(E)\neq0\iff\dim\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)=2$; [DR16, Conj. 3.12] specialised: $\kappa(f,g_\alpha,g^*_{\alpha^{-1}})\sim_L\log_p(P)Q-\log_p(Q)P$, the mixed classes vanish, and all four elliptic Stark values vanish. By [Cas23] the first conjecture is now equivalent to "$\dim\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)=2\Rightarrow\mathrm{Sel}\not\subset\ker\mathrm{loc}_p$", a statement about Ш.
* `[GAP C.1]` (the gap that would give BSD(rank) in analytic rank $2$ via this route): under the hypotheses of [Cas23, Thm. 5.2.3], $r_{\mathrm{an}}(E)=2\Rightarrow\kappa_p(E)\neq0$. By Proposition 4.9 it is equivalent to the $p^\infty$-Selmer form of the conclusion; the construction gives no independent purchase on it.

## 5. Higher-rank Euler systems and what a "rank-two Euler system for $T_pE$ over $\mathbb{Q}$" would have to be

### 5.1 The rank of an Euler system is determined by the representation

`[THEOREM]`/definitions (Perrin-Riou [PR98]; Mazur–Rubin [MR04, MR16]; Burns–Sano [BS21]; Burns–Sakamoto–Sano [BSS-II, BSS-III, BSS-IV]). For a $p$-adic representation $T$ of $G_K$ and the canonical Selmer structure $\mathcal F_{\mathrm{can}}$, the *core rank* is $\chi(\mathcal F_{\mathrm{can}})=\sum_{v\mid\infty}\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb{Z}_p}H^0(K_v,T^*(1))$ ([MR04, Thm. 5.2.15] `[theorem number unverified]`; the analogous formula for the unramified structure is displayed in [BSS-III, Thm. 3.6(ii)]; for $T=T_pE$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ it is checked directly in the proof of Proposition 5.5(iii) below); Euler systems of rank $r$ are norm-compatible families in $\bigwedge^rH^1(K',T)$ (exterior power biduals in [BS21]); the higher Kolyvagin derivative sends rank-$r$ Euler systems to rank-$r$ Kolyvagin systems, whose module is free of rank one when $r=\chi(\mathcal F)$ [MR16, BSS-II], and these control the structure of Selmer groups [MR16, Thms. 8.7/8.9, 13.4] `[theorem numbers as printed in the two versions; see numdam]`. Perrin-Riou's conjecture predicts a rank-$d^+$ Euler system for an effective motive $M$ with $d^+=\dim M_B^{c=1}$; the only known examples have $d^+=1$ (cyclotomic units, elliptic units, Kato's zeta elements, Beilinson–Flach, diagonal cycles), apart from Büyükboduk–Lei's rank-two Euler system for $\mathrm{Sym}^2f$ with $a_p(f)=0$, $p\ge7$ [BL19] (non-triviality there is guaranteed by the non-vanishing of the leading term of the relevant $L$-value and of a $p$-adic period modulo $p$, per the abstract).

For $T=T_pE$ over $\mathbb{Q}$: $T^*(1)\simeq T$ (Weil pairing), $H^0(\mathbb{R},T_pE)=T_pE^{c=1}$ has rank $1$, so $\chi(\mathcal F_{\mathrm{can}})=1$ — explicitly [BSS-III, §6.1]: "$r_T:=\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb{Z}_p}Y_\mathbb{Q}(T)=\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb{Z}_p}H^0(\mathbb{R},T)=1$". Over an imaginary quadratic $K$, $\chi=2$; over a totally real field of degree $d$, $\chi=d$.

**Consequence.** A "rank-two Euler system for $T_pE$ over $\mathbb{Q}$" is not the object the theory calls for; the Mordell–Weil rank of $E(\mathbb{Q})$ is *not* the rank of the Euler system. The rank-two *phenomenon* over $\mathbb{Q}$ must therefore be a *derived* phenomenon inside the rank-one theory. This is exactly what happens:

### 5.2 The rank-one Kolyvagin system of Kato already controls rank-two Selmer groups over $\mathbb{Q}$

`[THEOREM]` (Mazur–Rubin [MR04, Thm. 5.2.12(v)], as recalled in [BCGS26, Cor. C]; Kato [Kat04]; Burungale–Castella–Grossi–Skinner [BCGS26, Thm. C, Cor. C], Cambridge J. Math., to appear.) Let $E/\mathbb{Q}$ be non-CM, $p$ odd good ordinary with $E(\mathbb{Q}_p)[p]=0$, and assume the rational cyclotomic Main Conjecture (known for $p>3$ under (irr) by [Kat04, SU14, Wan] and for the Eisenstein cases treated in [CGS25]). Kolyvagin's derivative process applied to Kato's Euler system gives a Kolyvagin system $\{\kappa^{\mathrm{Kato}}_n\in H^1(\mathbb{Q},T/I_nT)\}_{n\in\mathcal N^{\mathrm{Kato}}}$ with $\kappa^{\mathrm{Kato}}_1=z_{\mathrm{Kato}}$; it is **nonzero**, and
$$\operatorname{ord}(\kappa^{\mathrm{Kato}}):=\min\{\nu(n):\kappa^{\mathrm{Kato}}_n\neq0\}=r_{\mathrm{str}}(E/\mathbb{Q}):=\operatorname{corank}_{\mathbb{Z}_p}H^1_{\mathrm{str}}(\mathbb{Q},E[p^\infty]),$$
the corank of the fine Selmer group. [CS26] (Castella–Sano, arXiv:2601.14504) extends the refined (divisibility-index) statements to primes of any reduction type for Kurihara's conjecture and to inert primes for Kolyvagin's.

**Corollary 5.3** (of [MR04, Thm. 5.2.12(v)]; elementary consequence). Under the hypotheses making $(T_pE,\mathcal F_{\mathrm{can}})$ satisfy the Mazur–Rubin hypotheses (e.g. $p\ge5$, $\bar\rho_{E,p}$ surjective; cf. [MR04, §6.2], [BSS-III, §6.3]): if $\operatorname{rank}E(\mathbb{Q})\ge2$ then **every** Kolyvagin system $\kappa$ for $(T_pE,\mathcal F_{\mathrm{can}})$ has $\kappa_1=0$. In particular $z_{\mathrm{Kato}}=0$ (Perrin-Riou's Conjecture [PR93, Conj. 3.3.2] in the form recalled in [BSS-III, Prop. 6.5(ii)]), and the bottom class of *any* Euler system for $T_pE$ over abelian extensions of $\mathbb{Q}$ that produces a Kolyvagin system — Kato's, a Beilinson–Flach system with Eisenstein $g$, or the trivial-representation projection of a Yoshida-type $\mathrm{GSp}_4$ or $\mathrm{GU}(2,1)$ system, should such projections form Euler systems — vanishes in rank $\ge2$.
*Proof.* If $\operatorname{rank}E(\mathbb{Q})\ge2$ then $\ker\big(E(\mathbb{Q})\otimes\mathbb{Q}_p/\mathbb{Z}_p\to E(\mathbb{Q}_p)\otimes\mathbb{Q}_p/\mathbb{Z}_p\big)$ has corank $\ge1$ because $E(\mathbb{Q}_p)\otimes\mathbb{Q}_p/\mathbb{Z}_p\simeq\mathbb{Q}_p/\mathbb{Z}_p$; this kernel lies in the fine Selmer group, so $r_{\mathrm{str}}\ge1$. By [MR04, Thm. 5.2.12(v)], for a nonzero Kolyvagin system $\operatorname{ord}(\kappa)=r_{\mathrm{str}}\ge1$, so $\kappa_1=0$; for $\kappa=0$ trivially. $\square$

So: over $\mathbb{Q}$, rank-two Selmer groups **are** controlled by explicit classes — the derived classes $\kappa^{\mathrm{Kato}}_\ell$ ($\nu(\ell)=1$, $\ell$ a Kolyvagin prime), which are nonzero by [BCGS26] when $r_{\mathrm{str}}=1$ (i.e. $\operatorname{rank}E(\mathbb{Q})=2$, Ш$[p^\infty]$ finite) — and Mazur–Rubin's structure theorem reads off the elementary divisors of $\mathrm{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/\mathbb{Q})^\vee$ from them. Their analytic counterparts are **Kurihara's numbers** $\tilde\delta_m$ (modular-symbol combinations $\sum_a\mathrm{Re}[a/m]/\Omega^+\cdot\prod_{\ell\mid m}\log_{\mathbb{F}_\ell}(a)$, [Kur14, (1.2)]): `[CONDITIONAL]` ([Kur14, Thm. 1.1.1], under the cyclotomic main conjecture and nondegeneracy of the $p$-adic height; unconditional refinements in [Kur14, Thms. 1.2.3, 1.2.5], [Kim24], [BCGS26, Rem. 3.3.4]) the ideals $\Theta_i(\mathbb{Q})^{(\delta)}=(\tilde\delta_m:\nu(m)\le i)$ satisfy $\Theta_0=\dots=\Theta_{r-1}=0\neq\Theta_r$ where $r=\operatorname{corank}\mathrm{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/\mathbb{Q})$, and they determine the $\mathbb{Z}_p$-module structure of $\mathrm{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/\mathbb{Q})^\vee$. Thus the "analytic side" of rank two over $\mathbb{Q}$ in the Euler-system world is the *Mazur–Tate element* $\tilde\theta_{\mathbb{Q}(\mu_m)}$ — its vanishing modulo $I^2$ and non-vanishing modulo $I^3$ — not $L''(E,1)$. Relating $\tilde\delta_{\ell_1\ell_2}$ to $L''(E,1)$ is the refined Mazur–Tate conjecture, open (Approach A/F).

### 5.3 Candidates from bigger groups

* $\mathrm{GSp}_4$: Loeffler–Skinner–Zerbes [LSZ22] construct Euler systems for the $4$-dimensional spin representations of cohomological cuspidal $\pi$; Loeffler–Zerbes [LZ26] prove the explicit reciprocity law and Bloch–Kato in analytic rank $0$ for their critical twists; [LZ21] apply this to modular abelian surfaces ($A(\mathbb{Q})$ finite and Ш$[p^\infty]$ finite when $L(A,1)\neq0$, under smoothness of the eigenvariety and an auxiliary odd twist), and [LZ24] show all these classes are multiples of a universal class. `[CONDITIONAL]`/remark: an endoscopic (Yoshida) $\pi$ attached to $(f,f')$ has spin representation $V_f\oplus V_{f'}$; even if the LSZ construction applied to such $\pi$ (it is formulated for cohomological cuspidal $\pi$ and the relevant Whittaker/period data; endoscopic forms are not covered by the Bloch–Kato applications, which need "big image"), the projection to $H^1(\mathbb{Q},V_f)$ would be an Euler system for $T_pE$ over $\mathbb{Q}$, hence of rank one, hence subject to Corollary 5.3: no help in rank $\ge2$. The same applies to $\mathrm{GU}(2,1)$ [LSZ-GU]. (Symmetric-power systems such as [LZ23-cube] are irrelevant here: $\mathrm{Sym}^3V_pE$ is irreducible and does not contain $V_pE$.)
* Loeffler's norm-relation machinery [Loe21] (spherical varieties) and the anticyclotomic Euler systems of [ACR23], [CD25], [Mar26] give genuine *anticyclotomic* Euler systems over imaginary quadratic $K$ from diagonal cycles; these are the systems behind [Cas23]. They are rank-one systems for Selmer structures with a $\mathfrak p/\bar{\mathfrak p}$-asymmetric local condition (the "one-sided" Selmer groups $\mathrm{Sel}_{\emptyset,0}$, $\mathrm{Sel}_{0,\emptyset}$ of [Cas23, (5.6)–(5.7)]), which is precisely how a class in the fine Selmer group of $E/\mathbb{Q}$ can arise as a bottom class without contradicting Corollary 5.3.
* [LZ25] (ultra-Kolyvagin systems) and [ABCL25] extend the machinery to non-ordinary settings; they do not change the rank count.

### 5.4 A derived Kato class in every rank `[NEW]`

Let $p\ge5$ be good ordinary, assume $\bar\rho_{E,p}$ surjective, and use the following inputs.

* (K1) `[THEOREM]` (Kato [Kat04, §12–13], Rohrlich.) $H^2(\mathbb{Z}[1/Np],T\otimes\Lambda)$ is $\Lambda$-torsion, hence $H^1_{\mathrm{Iw}}:=H^1(\mathbb{Z}[1/Np],T\otimes\Lambda)$ is torsion-free of $\Lambda$-rank one ($T=T_pE$, $\Lambda=\mathbb{Z}_p[[\Gamma]]$, $\Gamma=\mathrm{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}_\infty/\mathbb{Q})$); after $\otimes\mathbb{Q}_p$ it is free of rank one over the principal ideal domain $\Lambda_\mathbb{Q}:=\Lambda\otimes\mathbb{Q}_p$.
* (K2) `[THEOREM]` (Kato [Kat04, Thm. 12.5, §16] `[theorem numbers unverified]`.) There is a nonzero $z^{\mathrm{Iw}}\in H^1_{\mathrm{Iw}}\otimes\mathbb{Q}_p$ and a $\Lambda_\mathbb{Q}$-linear Coleman map $\mathrm{Col}:H^1_{\mathrm{Iw}}(\mathbb{Q}_p,T)\otimes\mathbb{Q}_p\to\Lambda_\mathbb{Q}$ (through the unramified quotient $T^-$) with $\mathrm{Col}(\mathrm{loc}_p z^{\mathrm{Iw}})=L_p(E,T)\cdot u$, $u\in\Lambda_\mathbb{Q}^\times$, and $\mathrm{Col}(x)(0)=c_{E,p}\cdot\exp^*_\omega(\mathrm{loc}_p x_0)$ with $c_{E,p}\neq0$ for every $x\in H^1_{\mathrm{Iw}}\otimes\mathbb{Q}_p$ (interpolation at the trivial character; $x_0$ the image of $x$ in $H^1(\mathbb{Q},V)$).
* (PR-der) `[THEOREM]` (Perrin-Riou [PR93]; Rubin [Rub94] `[statement standard, theorem numbers unverified]`.) If $\mathrm{Col}(x)(0)=0$, so that $\mathrm{loc}_px_0\in H^1_f(\mathbb{Q}_p,V)$, then $\frac{d}{dT}\mathrm{Col}(x)\big|_{T=0}=c'_{E,p}\cdot\log_\omega(\mathrm{loc}_px_0)$ with $c'_{E,p}\neq0$.

Write $V=V_pE$, $S=\{\ell\mid Np\}$, $T:=\gamma-1$ for a generator $\gamma$ of $\Gamma$.

**Proposition 5.5** `[NEW]`. Assume (K1), (K2). Let $m:=\max\{n\ge0: z^{\mathrm{Iw}}\in T^n\cdot H^1_{\mathrm{Iw}}\otimes\mathbb{Q}_p\}$ (finite since $z^{\mathrm{Iw}}\neq0$), write $z^{\mathrm{Iw}}=T^m z^{(m)}$, and let $z^{\mathrm{der}}\in H^1(\mathbb{Z}[1/S],V)$ be the image of $z^{(m)}$ under specialisation at the trivial character. Let $r_p:=\operatorname{ord}_{T=0}L_p(E,T)$. Then:
(i) $z^{\mathrm{der}}\neq0$, and $m\le r_p$;
(ii) $z^{\mathrm{der}}\in\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V)$ iff $m<r_p$;
(iii) if $\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V)\not\subset\ker(\mathrm{loc}_p)$ (e.g. if $\operatorname{rank}E(\mathbb{Q})\ge1$), then $H^1(\mathbb{Z}[1/S],V)=\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V)$; hence $z^{\mathrm{der}}$ is a nonzero Selmer class and $m<r_p$;
(iv) assuming also (PR-der): $\mathrm{loc}_p(z^{\mathrm{der}})\neq0$ iff $m=r_p-1$; in particular if $r_p=m+1$ then $z^{\mathrm{der}}\notin\mathrm{Sel}_0(\mathbb{Q},V)$.

*Proof.* (i) The sequence $0\to T\otimes\Lambda\xrightarrow{\ \cdot T\ }T\otimes\Lambda\to T_pE\to0$ of $G_{\mathbb{Q}}$-modules gives $H^1_{\mathrm{Iw}}\xrightarrow{\cdot T}H^1_{\mathrm{Iw}}\to H^1(\mathbb{Z}[1/S],T_pE)$ exact in the middle, so $H^1_{\mathrm{Iw}}/T\hookrightarrow H^1(\mathbb{Z}[1/S],T_pE)$, and the same after $\otimes\mathbb{Q}_p$. By maximality of $m$, $z^{(m)}\notin T\cdot H^1_{\mathrm{Iw}}\otimes\mathbb{Q}_p$, so $z^{\mathrm{der}}\neq0$. By $\Lambda_\mathbb{Q}$-linearity, $T^m\mathrm{Col}(\mathrm{loc}_pz^{(m)})=L_p(E,T)u$ in the domain $\Lambda_\mathbb{Q}$, so $T^m\mid L_p(E,T)$ and $\mathrm{Col}(\mathrm{loc}_pz^{(m)})=T^{-m}L_p(E,T)u$; thus $m\le r_p$.
(ii) By (K2), $\exp^*_\omega(\mathrm{loc}_pz^{\mathrm{der}})=c_{E,p}^{-1}\,(T^{-m}L_pu)(0)$, which vanishes iff $m<r_p$. Since $\exp^*_\omega$ induces an isomorphism $H^1(\mathbb{Q}_p,V)/H^1_f(\mathbb{Q}_p,V)\to\mathbb{Q}_p$ (one-dimensional singular quotient for good ordinary $E$), $\mathrm{loc}_pz^{\mathrm{der}}\in H^1_f(\mathbb{Q}_p,V)$ iff $m<r_p$. At $\ell\neq p$ nothing is to be checked: $H^1(\mathbb{Q}_\ell,V)=0$ because the local Euler characteristic is $0$ and $H^0(\mathbb{Q}_\ell,V)=0=H^2(\mathbb{Q}_\ell,V)\simeq H^0(\mathbb{Q}_\ell,V^*(1))^\vee=H^0(\mathbb{Q}_\ell,V)^\vee$ ($E(\mathbb{Q}_\ell)[p^\infty]$ is finite and $V^*(1)\simeq V$). Hence $\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V)=\ker\big(H^1(\mathbb{Z}[1/S],V)\to H^1(\mathbb{Q}_p,V)/H^1_f\big)$, proving (ii).
(iii) Tate's global Euler characteristic formula gives $\dim H^1(\mathbb{Z}[1/S],V)-\dim H^2(\mathbb{Z}[1/S],V)=\dim V-\dim H^0(\mathbb{R},V)=1$ ($H^0(\mathbb{Q},V)=0$). Poitou–Tate with $H^2(\mathbb{Q}_\ell,V)=0$ for all $\ell\in S$ and $H^1(\mathbb{Q}_\ell,V)=0$ for $\ell\neq p$ identifies $H^2(\mathbb{Z}[1/S],V)^\vee$ with $\ker\big(H^1(\mathbb{Z}[1/S],V)\to H^1(\mathbb{Q}_p,V)\big)=\mathrm{Sel}_0(\mathbb{Q},V)$. So $\dim H^1(\mathbb{Z}[1/S],V)=1+\dim\mathrm{Sel}_0(\mathbb{Q},V)$, while $\dim\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V)=\dim\mathrm{Sel}_0(\mathbb{Q},V)+\dim\mathrm{loc}_p(\mathrm{Sel})$ with $\mathrm{loc}_p(\mathrm{Sel})\subset H^1_f(\mathbb{Q}_p,V)$, which is one-dimensional. Hence $H^1(\mathbb{Z}[1/S],V)=\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V)$ iff $\mathrm{loc}_p(\mathrm{Sel})\neq0$. Under this hypothesis $z^{\mathrm{der}}\in\mathrm{Sel}$, and $m<r_p$ by (ii).
(iv) By (PR-der) applied to $x=z^{(m)}$ when $m<r_p$: $\log_\omega(\mathrm{loc}_pz^{\mathrm{der}})=c'^{-1}_{E,p}\frac{d}{dT}(T^{-m}L_pu)|_{T=0}$, which is nonzero iff $\operatorname{ord}_{T=0}T^{-m}L_p=1$, i.e. $m=r_p-1$. $\square$

**Remarks.** (1) In rank one (with the hypotheses of [BDV22]/Büyükboduk–Pollack–Sasaki on Perrin-Riou's conjecture), $z_{\mathrm{Kato}}\neq0$, so $m=0$ and $z^{\mathrm{der}}=z_{\mathrm{Kato}}\sim\log_p(P)P$. (2) In rank $\ge2$, Corollary 5.3 gives $m\ge1$: $z^{\mathrm{der}}$ is a genuinely *derived* class. (3) Kato's divisibility plus Mazur's control theorem give $r_p\ge\operatorname{corank}\mathrm{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/\mathbb{Q})$; if $\operatorname{rank}E(\mathbb{Q})=2$, Ш$[p^\infty]$ finite and $r_p=2$ (the $p$-adic BSD rank statement, open), then $m=1$ and by (iv) $\mathrm{loc}_p(z^{\mathrm{der}})\neq0$.

**Corollary 5.6** `[NEW]`, `[CONDITIONAL]` (rank-two Selmer basis over $\mathbb{Q}$). Assume the hypotheses of Proposition 5.5 including (PR-der), the hypotheses of [Cas23, Thm. 5.2.3] for some $(K,\chi)$, $\dim_{\mathbb{Q}_p}\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)=2$, $\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)\not\subset\ker\mathrm{loc}_p$, and $\operatorname{ord}_{T=0}L_p(E,T)=2$. Then
$$\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)=\mathbb{Q}_p\,\kappa_p(E)\ \oplus\ \mathbb{Q}_p\,z^{\mathrm{der}},\qquad \kappa_p(E)\text{ spans }\ker(\mathrm{loc}_p),\qquad \log_\omega(\mathrm{loc}_pz^{\mathrm{der}})=c'^{-1}_{E,p}\,\tfrac12L_p''(E,0)\,u(0)\neq0 .$$
*Proof.* $\kappa_p(E)\neq0$ spans $\ker\mathrm{loc}_p$ by [Cas23, Thm. 5.2.3]; $z^{\mathrm{der}}\in\mathrm{Sel}$ by Prop. 5.5(iii); $m\ge1$ by Corollary 5.3 (rank $\ge2$ is not assumed here, but $\dim\mathrm{Sel}=2$ with $\mathrm{loc}_p\neq0$ gives $\dim\mathrm{Sel}_0=1$, hence $r_{\mathrm{str}}\ge1$ and $\kappa_1^{\mathrm{Kato}}=z_{\mathrm{Kato}}=0$ by [MR04, Thm. 5.2.12(v)], so $m\ge1$); with $r_p=2$ we get $m=1$ and $\mathrm{loc}_pz^{\mathrm{der}}\neq0$ by (iv). Two classes, one in $\ker\mathrm{loc}_p$ and one not, are independent. $\square$

This is the analogue over $\mathbb{Q}$ of Castella's basis $(\kappa_p,x^{(2)}_{\mathfrak p})$ for CM curves [Cas26, Thm. C]. It illustrates the general pattern of §4–5: everything is detected by *$p$-adic* $L$-functions ($\Theta_{f/K}$ for $\kappa_p(E)$, $L_p(E,T)$ for $z^{\mathrm{der}}$) and by $p$-adic BSD-type hypotheses ($\rho=2$, $r_p=2$); nothing is detected by $L''(E,1)$.

## 6. The gaps, with the attempts made on each

**[GAP C.1] (analytic nonvanishing of the rank-two class).** *Hypotheses:* $E/\mathbb{Q}$, $p>3$ good ordinary, (h1)–(h3) and $(K,\chi)$ as in [Cas23, Thm. 5.2.3]; $r_{\mathrm{an}}(E)=2$. *Conclusion:* $\kappa_p(E)\neq0$.
*Attempt.* By Proposition 4.9 the statement is equivalent to $\operatorname{corank}\mathrm{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/\mathbb{Q})=2$ and $\mathrm{loc}_p(\mathrm{Sel})\neq0$; no property of the diagonal-cycle construction was found that is not already contained in this equivalence. Via [CH22, Thm. B] it would follow from [GAP C.2] together with $\operatorname{rank}E(\mathbb{Q})\ge1$, the latter being itself a weak form of BSD(rank). *Outcome:* the gap is a reformulation of the target statement; it cannot be attacked from inside this approach.

**[GAP C.2] (double zero of the anticyclotomic theta element).** *Hypotheses:* as in [CH22, Thm. A]; $r_{\mathrm{an}}(E)=2$. *Conclusion:* $\operatorname{ord}_J\Theta_{f/K}=2$, equivalently (given $\dim\mathrm{Sel}=2$) $h^{(2)}(P,Q)\neq0$ for a basis $P,Q$ of $\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)$.
*Attempt.* §4.6(i) proves $\operatorname{ord}_J\Theta_{f/K}\ge2$ from the $\tau$-antisymmetry of $h^{(1)}$ — a purely structural forced double zero. Equality is a nondegeneracy statement for an alternating $p$-adic pairing; no method relating it to complex $L$-derivatives exists (the interpolation property of $\Theta_{f/K}$ sees only order zero). *Outcome:* precise reformulation as nondegeneracy of $h^{(2)}$; verified numerically in [CH22, Appendix] for $21$ curves.

**[GAP C.3] (local triviality for non-CM $g$).** *Hypotheses:* rank $(2,0)$ setting of §4.1 with $\mathrm{Ad}^0V_g$ irreducible, DR16 hypotheses (I)–(IV). *Conclusion:* $\mathrm{loc}_p\kappa_p(E)=0$, i.e. $R_{\alpha\alpha}=0$ in Lemma 4.3.
*Attempt.* The CM proof uses the $\mathfrak p/\bar{\mathfrak p}$ asymmetry of the anticyclotomic deformation and has no analogue. The route suggested by [DR16, Rem. 3.5] (weight derivative of $\mathcal L^f_p$ along $(k,1,1)$) would require (a) a derivative-of-reciprocity-law formula at a non-exceptional point and (b) a factorisation of $\mathcal L^f_p(\mathbf f,g,g^*)|_{(k,1,1)}$; neither is available. *Outcome:* the prediction "$\operatorname{ord}_{k=2}\mathcal L^f_p(k,1,1)\ge2$" of §4.4 is the sharpest checkable statement extracted; open.

**[GAP C.4] (existence of admissible exotic $g$).** *Hypotheses:* $E/\mathbb{Q}$ with $r_{\mathrm{an}}(E)=2$. *Conclusion:* there exists $g\in S_1(N_g,\chi)$ with projective image $A_4,S_4$ or $A_5$, $\gcd(N_g,N_E)=1$, $p\nmid N_g$, $\alpha_g\neq\beta_g$, and $L(E,\mathrm{Ad}^0V_g,1)\neq0$.
*Attempt.* For CM $g$ the analogous statement is [BFH90]+[Vat03] ([CH22, Rem. 1.8]). For $E\otimes\mathrm{Ad}^0V_g$ (a $\mathrm{GL}_2\times\mathrm{GL}_3$ central value at the edge of the critical strip) we found no nonvanishing theorem in families of weight-one forms. *Outcome:* open; expected to hold for "most" $g$ but unproved.

**[GAP C.5] (Sha for $E\otimes\varrho_1\otimes\varrho_2$ in rank zero).** *Hypotheses:* [DR17] hypotheses, $L(E,\varrho_{gh},1)\neq0$. *Conclusion:* Ш$(E/H)^{\varrho_{gh}}[p^\infty]$ finite (equivalently $\mathrm{Sel}(\mathbb{Q},V_{fgh})=0$).
*Attempt.* Requires an Euler system (tame norm relations) for $V_f\otimes V_g\otimes V_h$ with $g,h$ cuspidal weight one; the anticyclotomic systems of [ACR23, CD25] cover CM twists; [Loe21] is the natural machinery for $\mathrm{GL}_2\subset\mathrm{GL}_2^3$. *Outcome:* open for non-CM $g,h$; in the adjoint case this is exactly the missing input "$\kappa^{\mathrm{ad}}=0$" of §4.3.

**[GAP C.6] (Gross–Kudla for $E/\mathbb{Q}$).** *Hypotheses:* $f,g,h$ weight $2$, $N>1$, global sign $-1$. *Conclusion:* the term $\sum_{p\mid N}c_p\log p$ in [YZZ23, Thm. 1.3.3] vanishes.
*Attempt:* none possible here; recorded because any complex-height approach to rank two via Chow–Heegner points ([DRS12]) needs it. Note that even granting it, Chow–Heegner points are torsion in rank $\ge2$ (their height is $L'(f\otimes g\otimes g,2)=L'(E,1)L(E\otimes\mathrm{Sym}^2g,2)+\dots=0$ when $r_{\mathrm{an}}(E)\ge2$), so this route is rank-one in nature.

**[GAP C.7] (a complex formula for the fine Selmer line).** *Hypotheses:* $\operatorname{rank}E(\mathbb{Q})=2$, Ш finite. *Conclusion sought:* any identity relating the canonical element $\mathrm{Log}_p(P\wedge Q)\in\mathrm{Sel}_0(\mathbb{Q},V_pE)$ (or its $\mathfrak p$-adic derived-height invariants) to $L''(E,1)$.
*Attempt.* The only global invariants of $\kappa_p(E)$ are $p$-adic heights; the BSD regulator is $\hat h(P)\hat h(Q)-\langle P,Q\rangle^2$; no pairing evaluates a $p$-adic-log-weighted combination of points to a real number. *Outcome:* structural obstruction; no formula of this type is plausible without a new "plectic" mechanism (cf. Nekovář–Scholl, cited in [DR16, §1] as the "more optimistic direction").

## 7. What this approach contributes to the programme

* It establishes (as a matter of record, with exact hypotheses) that the diagonal-cycle theory has produced the **only** canonical explicit Selmer classes for rank-two elliptic curves over $\mathbb{Q}$, that these lie in the fine Selmer group, and that their nonvanishing is a theorem-level *equivalent* of the $p$-Selmer rank being two (Castella 2023) — so they certify rank two but cannot be used to derive it from $L(E,s)$.
* It identifies the one point where analytic information could enter — the double zero of the anticyclotomic $\Theta_{f/K}$ (`[GAP C.2]`) — and shows that this is a $p$-adic nondegeneracy statement with no known bridge to $L''(E,1)$.
* It shows that "higher-rank Euler systems" are the wrong frame over $\mathbb{Q}$: the rank is $1$, the rank-two Selmer structure is controlled by derived Kolyvagin classes (now known to be nonzero) and by Kurihara's modular-symbol invariants; the analytic object controlling rank two over $\mathbb{Q}$ in this frame is the Mazur–Tate element modulo $I^3$, not the second derivative of $L(E,s)$. This should be cross-referenced with `A-iwasawa-padic.md` (p-adic BSD, $r_p$ vs $r_{\mathrm{alg}}$) and `F-analytic-side.md`.
* `[NEW]` items for adversarial review: Lemma 3.1 (multiplicity), Lemma 4.3 (bookkeeping of the uncontrolled coordinate), Proposition 4.9 (equivalence), Proposition 5.5 and Corollary 5.6 (derived Kato class and conditional rank-two basis). None of them proves any case of BSD; Proposition 5.5(i)–(iii) is unconditional under Kato's hypotheses and gives a canonical nonzero Selmer class in every rank (assuming $\mathrm{loc}_p\neq0$ on $\mathrm{Sel}$), which may be useful to `H-sha-and-descent.md`.
