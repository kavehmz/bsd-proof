## 2. The Sen calculation and its exact exclusion scope

Use the Sen convention Θ(Q_p(1))=1. Thus a summand C_p(j) has Sen
eigenvalue j; under the common geometric Hodge–Tate-weight convention
its weight is −j. This fixes the sign before using the word “weight”.

**[THEOREM, primary Sen input]** Berger–Colmez,
[Théorie de Sen et vecteurs localement analytiques, §1.2, Theorem 1.3 and Remark 1.4](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.24033/asens.2300.pdf),
Ann. ENS 49 (2016), 947–970, DOI 10.24033/asens.2300,
identify the finite cyclotomic descent space and its infinitesimal
operator. After extension to C_p, its zero eigenspace is generated by
Galois invariants. Applying this also to every Tate twist gives the
criterion: a representation is Hodge–Tate precisely when its Sen
operator is diagonalizable with integral eigenvalues. The operator
is compatible with tensor products. These facts are stated in the
primary research paper; its reference to Sen is not being used as a
substitute for inspecting the displayed formalism.

**[NEW] Proposition 2.1.** On U_γ and V⊗U_γ respectively,
$$\Theta_{U_\gamma}=-g_p^{-1}
\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\Theta_{V\otimes U_\gamma}
\sim
\begin{pmatrix}0&-g_p^{-1}\\0&0\end{pmatrix}
\oplus
\begin{pmatrix}1&-g_p^{-1}\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.\tag{3}$$
Neither representation is Hodge–Tate, even after restriction to a
finite extension of Q_p. The total dimension of the Hodge–Tate
invariant spaces of V⊗U_γ is two, whereas its dimension is four.

*Proof.* U_γ is trivial on the kernel of the cyclotomic character,
and its chosen Q_p-basis is cyclotomically finite. Write N e₀=e₁,
N e₁=0. Since N²=0,
$$\log\rho_{U_\gamma}(g)
=-\frac{\log_p\chi(g)}{g_p}N.$$
Dividing by log_pχ(g) gives the first formula, equivalently taking
the infinitesimal quotient by χ(g)−1 in Berger–Colmez's convention.
This nilpotent operator is nonzero.

The Hodge–Tate comparison for E gives Sen eigenvalues 0,1 on V,
each once. Differentiating the tensor action gives
Θ_V⊗1+1⊗Θ_U, proving the two blocks in (3) after scalar extension.
Each block has only one eigenvector direction. The invariant-space
formula applied to the Tate twists therefore gives one direction at
each of 0 and 1 and none elsewhere. On any finite extension the
cyclotomic image is still open and the same infinitesimal calculation
applies. In particular a finite extension cannot remove the nilpotent
part. ∎

The geometric input just used is
[Faltings, p-Adic Hodge Theory, III, Theorem 4.1](https://public.websites.umich.edu/~bhattb/almost_purity_2011/Faltings_-_p-adic-Hodge-theory.pdf),
JAMS 1 (1988), 255–299, DOI 10.1090/S0894-0347-1988-0924705-1.
The statement on printed p.298 covers smooth proper varieties with
a normal-crossing divisor removed. Hence a literal realization of
(2) or V⊗U_γ as a finite-dimensional geometric representation
covered by this comparison, or a subquotient of sums/tensors/Tate
twists of such representations, is excluded. The same exclusion
holds in any specified motivic category whose realizations are
Hodge–Tate. It is the coefficient object that is excluded.

This does **not** exclude the Selmer Bockstein, the class w₀, the
height pairing, an infinite pro-object, a derived arithmetic class,
or a different finite motive whose regulator produces the final
scalar. In particular the conclusion is not that w₀ is nongeometric.
A map on cohomology need not realize its coefficient deformation.

The old sign is preserved: on a constant lift f of a one-cocycle,
$$\partial f(g,h)=-c_\gamma(g)\,g f(h)T,
\qquad\beta=-\partial.\tag{4}$$
Nothing in (3) changes this cochain identity or the exact Euler factors.

## 3. The actual logarithm/Kummer replacement

**[THEOREM, motivic construction used here]** Huber–Kings,
[arXiv:1505.04574v1, Definitions 4.2.1 and 4.3.1, Lemma 4.2.3, Proposition 4.6.1 and Lemma 6.4.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1505.04574v1),
construct the Kummer motive of a section s of a smooth commutative
group G and its first logarithm motive. In their covariant convention
it has triangle H_G→K(s)→Q→H_G[1], with the last map the motivic
Kummer class. Its étale realization is the usual Kummer extension.
For G_m, H_G=Q(1); for E it realizes V_pE. The isogeny splitting
principle is Corollary 4.4.2. These constructions over number fields
need neither a Sha-finiteness premise nor a conjectural category of
all mixed motives.

For u∈F×, F a number field, the corresponding Deligne 1-motive is
$$M(u)=[\mathbb Z\xrightarrow{1\mapsto u}\mathbb G_m].\tag{5}$$
(The usual 1-motive degrees are −1,0; Huber–Kings use their displayed
shifted complex in DA.) Its p-adic realization has exact sequence
$$0\longrightarrow\mathbb Q_p(1)\longrightarrow V_pM(u)
\longrightarrow\mathbb Q_p\longrightarrow0.\tag{6}$$
For compatible p-power roots u_m and ε=(ζ_{p^m}), write
g(u_m)/u_m=ζ_{p^m}^{k_u(g)}. Its action on a lift e₀ of 1 and the
Tate basis e₁ is
$$g(e_1)=\chi(g)e_1,\qquad g(e_0)=e_0+k_u(g)e_1,
\quad k_u(gh)=k_u(g)+\chi(g)k_u(h).\tag{7}$$
In particular its connecting map sends 1 to δ_p(u), with a plus sign.
This is a genuine different-weight extension, not (2).

One can see (6) before any completion: its mod-p^m realization consists
of pairs (t,j) with t^{p^m}=u^j, modulo
(t,j)∼(t u^a,j+p^m a). The submodule j=0 is μ_{p^m}; the quotient
is Z/p^m. The pair (u_m,1) gives precisely (7).

The motivic weights in (6) are −2 and 0, with Sen eigenvalues 1 and
0. Since those eigenvalues are distinct, the Sen operator on this
two-dimensional extension is diagonalizable. A change of basis or
division of a period by g_p cannot turn them into the repeated
nilpotent eigenvalue in (3). More directly, any equivariant pushout
Q_p(1)→Q_p is zero: cyclotomic inertia acts nontrivially on its
source and trivially on its target.

The periods make this distinction concrete. With dz/z as toric
differential, the positive loop has period 2πi and a path 1→u has
period log(u), modulo 2πi. On p-adic units the latter becomes the
usual log_p(u). These are periods of the Q(1) subobject,
not Galois-invariant identifications of that subobject with Q_p.

For the elliptic curve the replacement is instead
$$M_E(R)=[\mathbb Z\xrightarrow{1\mapsto R}E],\qquad
0\to V_pE\to V_pM_E(R)\to\mathbb Q_p\to0.\tag{8}$$
Its motivic graded weights are −1 and 0; the abelian subobject has
rank two over Q, or rank one over K with its CM action. It is not
a one-dimensional rational Tate object. If R is torsion of order d,
the rational extension in (8) is zero because its Kummer class is
killed by d. The anchored isogeny splitting from Huber–Kings makes
this compatible with multiplication by d. This assertion is rational;
it does not assert integral splitting at p|d.

## 4. An actual motivic family on the CM curve and its exact trace

Use the same auxiliary integer a and rational function as before:
$$\Theta_a(X)=a^{-12}\Delta_E^{a^2-1}
\prod_{0\ne U\in E[a]}(x(X)-x(U))^{-6},\qquad
\operatorname{div}(\Theta_a)=12\left(a^2[O]-\sum_{U\in E[a]}[U]\right).
\tag{9}$$
Let U_a=E\setminus E[a]. Pull the first G_m logarithm motive back
along the actual morphism Θ_a:U_a→G_m. Denote the result by
K_Θ. This is a rational motivic object on the fixed CM curve;
its graded pieces remain Q and Q(1). It is not the first elliptic
logarithm, whose nonconstant graded piece is h₁(E).

Take the already constructed fields and sections
$$F_r=K(\mathfrak f p^r),\quad L_r=K(E[p^r]),\quad
R_r=\Omega_\infty/(f_0p^r),\quad
e_r(a)=N_{F_r/L_r}\Theta_a(R_r),\tag{10}$$
with f₀=39(1+i)³ and Δ_E=−64·39³. The coprimality of a and
the order of R_r puts R_r in U_a. These are the exact finite units
of the reviewed construction, with their existing norm relations.

**[NEW] Proposition 4.1 (motivic Kummer boundary and trace).**
Evaluation and transfer of K_Θ give exactly
$$\operatorname{Cor}_{F_r/L_r}\delta_{p^m}(\Theta_a(R_r))
=\delta_{p^m}(e_r(a)).\tag{11}$$
The same equality holds for the rational motivic Kummer maps and
for their log periods. There is no additional factor 12, degree,
or half in this operation: 12 is already in Θ_a.

*Proof.* Pullback of the diagonal Kummer complex to R_r is (5)
with u=Θ_a(R_r). For a finite extension F/L, the norm morphism
Res_{F/L}G_m→G_m sends the corresponding section u to N_{F/L}u.
Functoriality of the complex [Z→G] and its boundary therefore sends
its Kummer extension to that of the norm. Equivalently, after a
normal closure, adding the conjugate extension classes corresponds
to multiplying the conjugate units. This is exactly corestriction
of the cocycle from the pair construction following (7).
The group homomorphism identity δ(uv)=δ(u)+δ(v) proves (11)
integrally modulo every p^m and rationally. Integrating dz/z gives
the same sum-of-logarithms formula, modulo the usual Betti periods
and exactly for log_p on units. ∎

The curve residue of K_Θ is exactly the principal divisor in (9).
Its degree-zero Abel–Jacobi class is zero, because that divisor is
principal. Also R_r is torsion, so R_r*Log_E^(1) is rationally split
by (8). These calculations do not kill the unit in (11); its class
is a different-weight Kummer extension. They show why replacing the
unit's logarithm by the elliptic first-log fiber or its principal
divisor boundary does not create a Mordell–Weil derivative.

There is a second finite-level weight issue at the actual twist.
Write M_Ψ for the CM component of h₁(E), and
R=M_Ψ(−1), whose chosen p-component is
ρ=Ψχ_cyc^(−1)=(Ψ^c)^(−1). Tensoring (6) by R gives an extension
of R by M_Ψ, not an extension of Q by M_Ψ. Its motivic weights
are respectively +1 and −1. A rational Betti basis of R is not
a morphism Q→R. On every finite number field the corresponding
p-component has no invariant vector: it is the dual of the other
elliptic Tate component, and Frobenius at a good place has no
eigenvalue one. Thus the insertion of t_ρ modulo p^m at the large
division level, where it is invariant, cannot be replaced by an
invariant Q_p vector at one finite level. This records the precise
coefficient distinction in the actual Kummer replacement; it is not
an assertion that no different motivic trace could exist.

## 5. A concrete quadratic logarithm test

The most direct secondary candidate is Sym²K_Θ. Its graded pieces
are Q,Q(1),Q(2). In a geometric logarithm basis
(e₀²/2,e₀e₁,e₁²), continuation with additive logarithm l gives
the entries l and l²/2. The factorial is thus fixed by an actual
symmetrical-square construction, not inserted to match a derivative.

**[NEW] Proposition 5.1.** For each actual u=Θ_a(R_r), the
self-Yoneda product of its rational Kummer class has coefficient
Q_p(2), cohomological degree two, and is zero. A cochain
nullhomotopy is
$$d\left(-\frac{k_u(g)^2}{2}\right)(g,h)
=k_u(g)\chi(g)k_u(h)
=(\delta_p(u)\cup\delta_p(u))(g,h).\tag{12}$$
Consequently this secondary boundary is not the first class w₀
in H¹(Q,V), even though its period matrix contains log(u)²/2.

*Proof.* In the basis ε² of Q_p(2), the differential of
t(g)=−k_u(g)²/2 is
χ(g)²t(h)−t(gh)+t(g). Substitute (7) and expand the square:
the two pure squares cancel and the remaining term is precisely
the right side of (12). The same vanishing holds in rational
motivic cohomology by graded commutativity in degree one, since
2 is invertible. Sym²(6) supplies the displayed quadratic cochain;
it does not convert a noncocycle into an H¹ class or remove its
Tate twist. Transfer of the zero class remains zero. ∎

This tests the literal first quadratic logarithm operation. It says
nothing about all polylogarithm classes, unrelated higher symbols,
or secondary classes with extra prescribed nullhomotopies.

## 6. Positive construction: the derivative from finite Kummer data

There is nevertheless an exact way to extract w₀ from the actual
finite Kummer extensions. It retains integral torsion coefficients
and a kernel operation, rather than declaring (6) equal to (2).

Let M_m=E[p^m] and B_m=(Z/p^m)[T]/T². Choose a cyclotomic layer
Q_n with [Q_n:Q]=p^n and n≥m. The map
$$\theta_{n,m}:(\mathbb Z/p^m)[\Gamma/\Gamma^{p^n}]
\longrightarrow B_m,\qquad\gamma\longmapsto1+T\tag{13}$$
is well defined because (1+T)^{p^n}=1 in B_m. With the inverse
Galois action on the group algebra, its target action is exactly
g↦1−c_γ(g)T modulo p^m. No logarithm with an unfixed generator
normalization is introduced here.

Construct the class over Q_n from (11) with r≥max(m,n+1), first
inserting the already fixed coefficient t_ρ,m over L_r and then
corestricting. Use Shapiro over K and Q, retaining the full
cyclotomic group-ring coordinate. Divide by the same Kato factor
$$12\bigl(a^2-\Psi^c((a))\overline\sigma_a\bigr),\tag{14}$$
which is a unit in the integral group algebra in this prime scope,
and use the same γ_CM=2pr_ρ(γ_E⁺). Thus neither the opposite
Schmitt sign nor the rational Betti factor two is lost. Applying
(13) produces a well-defined class
$$\mathcal Z_m\in H^1(G_{\mathbb Q,S},M_m\otimes B_m).\tag{15}$$
It is the reduction of z∞ modulo (p^m,T²). Independence of r and
n follows from the saved exact norm relations and Shapiro/projection
compatibility. Increasing n in (13) does not change its value.

**[NEW] Proposition 6.1 (exact finite derived Kummer boundary).**
There is a unique class d_m∈H¹(G_{Q,S},M_m) such that
$$T d_m=\mathcal Z_m.\tag{16}$$
If a cocycle for (15) is f(g)=a(g)+Tb(g), and
a(g)=g v−v, then it is explicitly represented by
$$d_m(g)=b(g)+c_\gamma(g)g v.\tag{17}$$
The classes d_m are compatible as m increases and equal w₀ mod p^m.
Hence their inverse limit is exactly w₀, and d_m⊗T recovers
κ_p modulo p^m. No Selmer-corank or Sha-finiteness premise is used.

*Proof.* The coefficient sequence
$$0\to M_m\xrightarrow{x\mapsto Tx}M_m\otimes B_m
\xrightarrow{T\mapsto0}M_m\to0\tag{18}$$
is exact. Its map on H¹ from the left is injective, since
H⁰(G_{Q,S},M_m)=E(Q)[p^m]=0; the known rational torsion has
order two. By the already proved integral divisibility in (1),
the augmentation of (15) is zero in H¹. The long exact sequence
therefore proves existence and uniqueness in (16).

For the cochain formula use the standard left-action differential
df(g,h)=g f(h)−f(gh)+f(g). The cocycle condition for f gives
da=0 and db(g,h)=c_γ(g)g a(h). Since [a]=0, choose v as stated.
The deformed coboundary of v is
$$d_{B_m}v(g)=g v-v-c_\gamma(g)g v\,T.$$
Subtract it from f to obtain the T-multiple in (17). It is a
cocycle: d(c_γ(g)g v)(g,h)=−c_γ(g)g a(h), cancelling db.
The choice of v is unique because H⁰=0; changing f by a
coboundary changes (17) by a coboundary. This also follows from
the injection in (18).

Reduction of the equality z∞=Tw∞ modulo (p^m,T²) shows that
w₀ mod p^m satisfies (16); uniqueness identifies it with d_m.
This proves compatibility and the inverse-limit assertion without
postulating a compatible family of arbitrary cochain choices. ∎

Formula (17) is a genuine finite-unit computation of the derivative
as a cohomology class. An ordinary norm/boundary in §4 gives z_n;
the extra kernel extraction in (16), with the explicit correction
term and plus sign, is what gives w₀. It remains p-dependent.
The finite d_m are asserted here to be global cohomology classes;
no extra claim about every integral finite-level Selmer local
condition is needed. Their rational inverse-limit specialization
has the full-Selmer membership already proved for w₀.

## 7. Exact frame comparison and the attempted rational lift

For the known P,Q let H_p=(h_p(P_i,P_j)), l=(log_ωP,log_ωQ),
and retain the actual rational frame Ξ of
[CM-Biex](cm-biextension-cycle-attack.md#6-the-first-remaining-equality-with-its-rational-target).
Its realizations are R_∞(Ξ)=Reg_∞ and R_p(Ξ)=Reg_p.
The existing map on the one-dimensional frame line is
$$\mathcal B_p(\Xi)=
\frac{\operatorname{adj}(H_p)l}{g_p}\otimes T=:R_{p,\omega}.
\tag{19}$$
When Reg_p≠0 the height-orthogonal projection pr_W onto the
Mordell–Weil subspace is defined on the full Selmer space. Combining
(17) with the reviewed height identity gives the exact positive
comparison
$$\mathcal B_p^{-1}\left(
\frac{p}{2\#E(\mathbb F_p)}\operatorname{pr}_W
\bigl((\varprojlim_m d_m)\otimes T\bigr)\right)
=\frac{c_{\rm cmp,p}M_p}{4e_p\operatorname{Reg}_p}\Xi=:Z_p,
\tag{20}$$
where
$$e_p=(1-\alpha^{-1})^2,\qquad
c_{\rm cmp,p}=(156i\Omega_p)^{-1},\qquad
M_p=\int\log_p(\chi_{\rm cyc})^2\,d\mu_\psi.$$
This is not a new assumption: Proposition 6.1 identifies its input
with κ_p, so (20) is precisely the prior proved formula. The
unchanged identity k_α e_p=#E(F_p)/p accounts for every local
factor. At Reg_p=0 only the undivided height identity is used;
the inverse in (20) is not claimed to exist.

Here is the attempted rational lift, and where its direct operations
stop. The motive K_Θ and all evaluations in §4 exist over number
fields. Their rational Kummer boundary and trace are fully computed
by (11). They give the original unit class. The elliptic first-log
evaluation has zero rational extension at the torsion sections, and
the curve residue has zero Abel–Jacobi class. The quadratic
logarithm in §5 supplies the explicit zero self-cup in degree two
and Tate weight two, rather than (17). Tensoring the unit extension
by the CM motive changes its quotient to R, not to Q; the finite
mod-p^m coefficient insertion is not an invariant rational vector.

Replacing that finite construction by a fixed rational coefficient
extension realizing U_γ would fail Proposition 2.1. However, the
output of (17) has forgotten that non-Hodge–Tate coefficient
extension, so this obstruction does not prove that its scalar in
(20) cannot arise from a different secondary motive. In particular
one cannot deduce failure of rationality from the Sen calculation.
