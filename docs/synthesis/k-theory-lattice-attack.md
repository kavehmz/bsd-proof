# The actual ordinary K-theory lattice of the 389a1 arithmetic surface

Date: 2026-09-12. Owner: coordinator. New deductions below passed
[independent review](review-k-theory-lattice.md). The objective is full BSD over Q. This note constructs
an explicit finite integral lattice and its Chern-class map; it does not
assume that map captures the full Brauer group.

## 1. Model and inputs

Let X be the regular projective flat model
$$Y^2Z+YZ^2=X^3+X^2Z-2XZ^2$$
of E=389a1. Every geometric fiber is integral and reduced. The only
singular fiber is the split nodal cubic at 389. These statements,
Pic(X)=Z³ and Br(X)≅Sha(E/Q), including the real place, were proved
and independently reviewed in the
[arithmetic-surface note](weil-etale-lattice-attack.md).
The zero section is O. Put P=(-1,1), Q=(0,-1), the certified full basis.

**[THEOREM, source inputs]** The homotopy exact sequence applies to
a flat proper morphism of finite presentation with geometrically connected,
reduced fibers: [Stacks Project, Proposition 58.15.2](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BUM).
The geometric fundamental group of an elliptic curve in characteristic
zero is its full Tate module. This follows from the Riemann-existence
comparison and the covering theory of C/Γ; see
[Milne, Lectures on Étale Cohomology v2.21, §3, Theorem 3.4](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/LECc.pdf).
Identification by the multiplication-by-n covers is Galois-equivariant.
The rational point splits the generic arithmetic fundamental-group sequence.

Also π₁(Spec Z)=1: a connected finite étale cover would give a nontrivial
number field unramified at every finite prime, excluded by Minkowski.
The exact arithmetic statement is
[Milne, Algebraic Number Theory v3.08, Theorem 4.9](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/ANT.pdf).

## 2. A uniform Frobenius calculation kills the entire fundamental group

Exact finite-field counts give
$$\#E(\mathbb F_2)=5,\quad\#E(\mathbb F_3)=6,
\quad\#E(\mathbb F_5)=9.$$
The following reproduction uses only integer arithmetic:

```sh
python3 - <<'PY'
for p in (2,3,5):
    count=1+sum((y*y+y-x*x*x-x*x+2*x)%p==0
                for x in range(p) for y in range(p))
    print(p,count)
PY
```

It was executed and returned exactly the three counts above. All three
primes are good. For ℓ≠q, the Frobenius action on T_ℓE satisfies
$$\det(1-F_q)=q+1-a_q=\#E(\mathbb F_q).$$

**[NEW] Lemma 2.1.** The continuous Galois coinvariants of the full
geometric Tate module vanish:
$$\left(\prod_\ell T_\ell E\right)_{G_{\mathbb Q}}=0.$$

*Proof.* Fix ℓ. For ℓ not equal to 2 or 5, choose q=2, so
det(1−F_q)=5 is a unit in Z_ℓ. For ℓ=5 choose q=3, giving the unit 6;
for ℓ=2 choose q=5, giving the unit 9. In every case q≠ℓ and
1−F_q is an automorphism of T_ℓE. Consequently every vector in that
component is a Galois difference, so its coinvariants vanish.

For the profinite product, this assertion may be checked in every finite
quotient, which involves only finitely many primary components. Equivalently,
the closed subgroup generated by the differences contains each primary
component and hence their dense direct sum. Its closure is the full product.
This proves the continuous-coinvariant assertion. □

**[NEW] Proposition 2.2.** The full étale fundamental group of X is trivial.

*Proof.* Homotopy exactness for X→Spec Z, with the geometric generic fiber,
gives a surjection π₁(E_Qbar)→π₁(X), since the base fundamental group is
trivial. Thus π₁(X) is a quotient of the abelian group ∏_ℓ T_ℓE.

The generic zero-section image G_Q→π₁(E) maps trivially to π₁(X): it
factors through the integral zero section and π₁(Spec Z)=1. Conjugation
by that section is precisely the natural Galois action on the geometric
Tate module. Therefore the displayed surjection factors through its
continuous Galois coinvariants. Lemma 2.1 makes that quotient zero. □

This proves full simple connectedness in the étale sense, rather than
only the vanishing of the abelianization. It does not make higher
arithmetic cohomology zero.

## 3. The zero-cycle group and K₀ ring are explicit

**[THEOREM, arithmetic class field theory]** For a regular connected
scheme proper and flat over Z with projective generic fiber, unramified
class field theory identifies CH₀ with the modified abelian fundamental
group, in which real points split. The statement is recalled in
[Alexander Schmidt, arXiv:math/0204330v1, introduction](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0204330),
and its relative surface form is Theorem 2. The empty-boundary case
is ordinary CH₀, as explicitly stated after Definition 3.1.
No finite-Brauer or finite-Sha hypothesis occurs in this theorem.

**[NEW] Corollary 3.1.** CH₀(X)=0.

*Proof.* Its reciprocity target is a quotient of π₁(X), which is zero
by Proposition 2.2. Apply the stated isomorphism. □

This is the group of codimension-two cycles on the arithmetic surface,
not the divisor/point group on its generic elliptic curve.

**[NEW] Proposition 3.2.** Rank and determinant give a ring isomorphism
$$K_0(X)\simeq\mathbb Z\oplus\operatorname{Pic}(X)
\simeq\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z^3,$$
where the rank-zero ideal has square zero. In additive Picard notation,
the multiplication is
$$(r,L)(s,M)=(rs,sL+rM).$$

*Proof.* On this regular projective surface coherent sheaves admit finite
locally free resolutions. Rank and determinant are surjective, and their
kernel SK₀ is generated by classes of closed points. These facts are
given in [Weibel, K-book, II.8.1–8.2 and Example 8.2.2](https://www.math.rutgers.edu/~weibel/Kbook/Kbook.II.pdf).
Rational-equivalence relations among codimension-two cycles vanish under
the cycle map to this kernel. One can see this from the Quillen spectral
sequence: its E₂^(2,−2) term is CH₀, and its terminal filtration piece
surjects from that group. There is no filtration piece of codimension three.
Corollary 3.1 therefore makes SK₀ zero. Concretely, no additional Gersten
conjecture is needed: normalize an integral curve in X, a finite operation
because X is excellent. For div(f)=D₊−D₋ on that regular curve,
O(−D₊)≅O(−D₋), and the effective-divisor exact sequences make their
closed-point classes equal in G₀. Finite pushforward gives exactly the
rational-equivalence relation on X. Hence CH₀ surjects onto the
point-generated SK₀. The known Picard computation gives the displayed group.

The determinant of a tensor product is
det(V⊗W)=det(V)^(rank W)⊗det(W)^(rank V), also for virtual classes.
This proves the multiplication formula and the square-zero assertion. □

A concrete integral basis is
$$1,\quad [\mathcal O_X(O)]-1,\quad
[\mathcal O_X(P-O)]-1,\quad[\mathcal O_X(Q-O)]-1.$$
The horizontal sections here are Cartier divisors because X is regular.
Every product of the last three basis elements is zero in ordinary K₀.
No K-theoretic finite-generation conjecture was invoked.

## 4. The actual Chern-class map retains the original gap

Work in the fppf topology when n is not invertible on X. Kummer theory gives
$$0\longrightarrow\operatorname{Pic}(X)/n
\xrightarrow{c_1}H^2_{\rm fppf}(X,\mu_n)
\longrightarrow\operatorname{Br}(X)[n]\longrightarrow0.$$

**[NEW] Proposition 4.1.** For every n≥1 the map c₁ from K₀(X)/n has
kernel exactly its rank summand, and cokernel Sha(E/Q)[n]. Thus
$$0\longrightarrow\mathbb Z/n
\longrightarrow K_0(X)/n
\xrightarrow{c_1}H^2_{\rm fppf}(X,\mu_n)
\longrightarrow\operatorname{Sha}(E/\mathbb Q)[n]
\longrightarrow0.$$

*Proof.* The first Chern class is the first Chern class of the determinant.
By Proposition 3.2 determinant has only the rank summand as kernel, and
its induced map to Pic(X)/n is onto with the same kernel. Kummer injects
Pic(X)/n and identifies the displayed cokernel with Br(X)[n]. The already
reviewed arithmetic-surface identification supplies Sha. □

The finite integral lattice requested by the proposed approach therefore
exists explicitly: its mod-n group is (Z/n)^4. The natural map from that
lattice supplies the Picard part, and its full cokernel is precisely the
group the proof still needs to control. In particular:

- Surjectivity for every n would prove Sha(E)=0.
- A fixed nonzero integer m annihilating all these cokernels would prove
  Sha(E) finite, since Sha(E)[m] is finite by finite descent.
- The computed ring structure alone does not supply that surjectivity or m.

The same distinction holds for noncuspidal heights. The ordinary K₀
product of the two point-line classes is zero, whereas their arithmetic
intersection can retain nonzero metric data. An ordinary zero class is
not a specified arithmetic trivialization with the required regulator.

z∈H²_fppf(X,μ_n), for every n, to a virtual vector-bundle class v on X
